### 2c. ⭐ THE HONEST HEADLINE

**The pattern the individual reviews have flagged is real, it holds across the whole corpus, and
it is the single most important empirical finding this project has produced:**

> **The direction and the distance are frequently real. The specific numeric premise almost never
> holds as stated.**

The three most recent tests say it in three different ways, and each was pre-registered before its
runner existed:

- **`PT-041` (V13) — `PARTIALLY SUPPORTED`.** *"the distance is real, the promise is not."* The
  distance is emphatically not what a matched random entry gets; the **two specific numbers he
  gives are stated far more confidently than they hold.**

  drill's own premise — that a one-hour hold makes it the day's extreme — comes in at **0.3461 /

**Set against that, four claims clear their nulls and not one of them is an entry rule.** They are
