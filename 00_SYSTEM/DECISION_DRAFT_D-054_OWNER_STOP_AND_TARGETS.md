## 0. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH SUMMARY, INCLUDING THE PART THAT DOES NOT GO THE OWNER'S WAY

The owner has stated his own risk rule in the first person, unprompted and unhedged — **stop 25
pips beyond the perceived extreme, first target 30 pips, second target 50 pips.** That is the
**first complete, self-consistent, three-number risk template this project has ever been given**,
from any source, at any tier. Twenty-one lessons have not produced one.

⭐ **And two of the three numbers land on corpus evidence that was already there.** The **25**
sits exactly on the upper bound of `A-123`'s garbled *"15 25's… above the high below the low"* —
**and it resolves that record's `total` ambiguity in favour of the per-side reading**, which was
the factor-of-two question on the corpus's only spoken stop. The **50** is the number V04, V09 and
V10 all reach for as a target. And **25 → 50 is exactly the `2 to 1` V06 states in words.**

⛔ **But it is not course-verified, it does not close `A-123`, and one of its three numbers has no
corpus support at all.** The **30** appears nowhere in the corpus at any tier. More seriously:
**the rule's ANCHOR is an undefined term.** *"The perceived high"* is not defined by the owner,
not defined by the course, and is not the same object as *"the high"*. **A stop distance measured
from an undefined point is not a codable rule**, and this draft opens `A-132` to say so rather than
quietly substituting the swing high.

### 1.1 ⚠️ THE MIRRORED CASE IS AN INFERENCE AND IS FLAGGED, NOT ASSUMED

The owner stated **only the short side.** The symmetric long — *stop 25 pips below the perceived
low, TP1 30, TP2 50* — is the obvious reading and **this draft does not adopt it.**

**Why the caution is not pedantry here.** This corpus has a documented history of asymmetric
behaviour between the long and short cases: `C-022`'s *"creep up or creep down"* is asymmetric,
`D-051` §2 found `mm_adr`'s two markers move **monotonically outward** rather than symmetrically,
and `A-105`'s own slide pairs **red → SELL** with **green → BUY** as distinct capsules. **A method
that distinguishes the two directions everywhere else may distinguish them here.** Assuming
symmetry would be a `D-030` approximation dressed as common sense.

⭐ **One sentence from the owner closes this**, and §7 Q1 asks for it.

## 7. THE QUESTIONS FOR THE OWNER

1. ⭐⭐ **What is *"the perceived high"*?** The four candidates are in §4. **This is the single
   highest-value sentence you could supply in this session** — it is the only thing standing between
   a complete risk template and an executable one, and it closes `A-132` outright.
2. **Is the long side the mirror?** — *stop 25 below the perceived low, TP1 30, TP2 50*. §1.1 says
   why this is asked rather than assumed.
3. **Are TP1 and TP2 a scale-out or alternatives?** — i.e. part off at 30 and the rest at 50, or 30
   in one regime and 50 in another? The numbers alone do not say, and the answer changes every
   backtest that uses them.
4. ⭐ **Do these targets REPLACE the duration-based exits?** `C-001`, `C-016` and `A-121` all
   measure exits in **days**; yours are in **pips**. If price targets are what you actually exit on,
   that is a large result and §3.5 explains why this draft will not assume it.
5. **Is 25/30/50 what the course TAUGHT, or what you arrived at?** §2 assumes the latter — chiefly
   because `TP1 = 30` matches nothing in the corpus (§3.4). **If you are testifying to the
   teaching**, the status changes to `RESOLVED — OWNER ATTESTATION` and the entry is rewritten.
6. **Are the pips instrument-relative?** `A-005` records the trading-zone band as instrument-
   relative; 25/30/50 on `USDNOK` (the pair `MMM.tpl` was saved on) is not the same risk as on
   `EURUSD`.
