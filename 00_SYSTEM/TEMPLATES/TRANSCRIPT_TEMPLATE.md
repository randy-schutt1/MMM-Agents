## COVERAGE

```text

Covered: 00:00:00 – HH:MM:SS
Gaps:    (list any unavailable ranges and why)
```

A partial transcript must say so here. An unmarked partial reads as complete to the
reviewer.

## CONVENTIONS

- Timestamps `[HH:MM:SS]` at every topic change and at every rule statement.
- `[unclear]` — speech present but not intelligible.
- `[inaudible NN sec]` — a stretch that could not be recovered.
- `[?word?]` — best guess at a word; genuinely uncertain.
- `[on screen: ...]` — what is visible when it matters to the teaching.
- `[chart: ...]` — instrument / timeframe / what is being drawn.
- `[HOMEWORK]` — inline marker wherever the instructor assigns work.

**Never silently invent missing speech.** A marked gap is evidence; a smooth
fabrication is corruption.
