### Column guidance

- **What to notice** — direct the reader's eye. "The third push fails to exceed the
  second", not "a great example".
- **Rule supported** — the specific source-note or concept ID this image is
  evidence for (`S4`, `CL-003`). If it supports nothing specific, ask why it was
  captured.
- **Classification** — `SOURCE` (instructor is stating it), `VISUAL` (demonstrated
  on screen), `INFERRED` (the agent's reading of the image).

## CAPTURE LIMITATIONS

If frame extraction was unavailable and visual evidence is described rather than
captured, say so here explicitly and reference `SETUP_ISSUES.md` I-006. This
materially weakens the evidence base and the reviewer must know.

**Never substitute a generated, illustrative, or reconstructed image for a real
course screenshot.**
