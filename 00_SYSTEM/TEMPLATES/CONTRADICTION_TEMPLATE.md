# CONTRADICTION RECORD TEMPLATE

> Copy this block into `11_CONTRADICTIONS/CONTRADICTIONS.md` as a new `C-NNN`
> section. Do not create separate files.

**Never silently reconcile conflicting teachings.** A contradiction that is quietly
smoothed over becomes an invented rule that no one can trace.

---

## RULES

1. Log the conflict when you notice it, not after you have talked yourself out of
   it.
2. Do not guess. `UNRESOLVED` is a legitimate and often correct end state during
   the Student Phase.
3. Do not delete a record once resolved — append the resolution.
4. Re-examine open contradictions at every cumulative review (25/50/75/100%);
   later lessons frequently resolve earlier conflicts.
5. The reviewer treats an ignored contradiction as error code E13.
