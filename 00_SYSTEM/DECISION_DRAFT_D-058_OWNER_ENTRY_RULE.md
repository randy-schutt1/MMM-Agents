## 1. THE ATTESTATION, RECORDED VERBATIM AND UNINTERPRETED

> **Owner, 2026-08-15:** *"the entry is the close of the candle after the ema cross. But when your
> confidence and probably [probability] is on your side then you can take it on the second leg
> hammer, railroad tracks, doji, etc. then high level is placing a limit order on the close of the
> outside bar. That's the safest entry."*

**Parsed as three tiers, and the tiering is the owner's own — *"then high level"*, *"the safest"*:**

| Tier | Trigger | Order type | Owner's own label |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — default** | the **close of the candle AFTER the EMA cross** | (not stated — market on close, by implication) | *"the entry"* |
| **2 — confidence-conditional** | the **second-leg** candle signal: **hammer, railroad tracks, doji, etc.** | (not stated) | *"when your confidence and probability is on your side"* |
| **3 — "high level"** | the **close of the outside bar** | ⭐ **LIMIT order** — the only order type he names | *"high level… That's the safest entry"* |

⭐ **Tier 3 is the only tier with an order type, and it is the one he calls safest.** A limit at a
level already traded is a **retracement entry** — it does not fill unless price comes back. **That
is what makes it "safest": it trades fill probability for price.** ⚠️ *That is the reading this
draft finds most natural; the owner does not say it, and §5 Part 4 does not adopt it.*

### 1.1 ⚠️ What "etc." does, and why it is not tidied away

*"hammer, railroad tracks, doji, **etc.**"* is an **open list**. ⛔ **An open list is not a
specification.** The three named are single-bar reversal signals; *"etc."* admits an unbounded
family the owner has not enumerated. **`D-030` binds**: a session may not complete the list, and
tier 2 is `DO NOT CODE` on its trigger set alone, independently of everything else wrong with it.
