**Purpose.** `MASTERY_STANDARD.md` dimensions A (Recall), C (Discrimination), D (Sequence) and E
(Exceptions) are claims about understanding, and a mastery report asserting them is a
self-assessment. This probe makes the claim **falsifiable**: twelve questions, answered with the
reasoning shown, scored afterwards against V10's own words.

**Design note, stated in advance.** V09's probe scored 9/9 and its own write-up said that was
*"unimpressive on a lesson printed in plain English"*. **V10's questions are deliberately harder**:
four of the twelve (Q3, Q6, Q9, Q11) have an attractive wrong answer that this session had to
actively reject, and two (Q7, Q12) ask about **absences**, which are the answers a confident
reader is most likely to get wrong by supplying something plausible.

## Q1 — What does V10 designate as the student's "signature trade", and in exactly what words?

**Answer.** *"The safety trade with a second leg element"* — `[00:40:47]`. The designation is at
`[00:42:14]`–`[00:42:23]`: *"I'm officially designating everyone in here your signature trade as of
today … You're all now safety trade traders."*

**Reasoning trace.** The question at `[00:40:14]` is *"What is the best trade signal … what's my
signature trade?"* The answer is given after a self-conscious drumroll at `[00:40:40]`, phrased as
*"the best trade to take in any market condition — any market condition — is…"*. The qualifier
*"with a second leg element"* is part of the name, not a separate condition, because he repeats the
full phrase at `[00:41:03]`.

## Q2 — V10 gives a definition the corpus has waited nine lessons for. What is defined, and as what?

**Answer.** **Peak formation high / peak formation low**, defined as the **week's extreme** —
`[01:14:06]`: *"the highest point on a chart within the week, or the lowest point on the chart
within the week."* Equivalently, PFH = HOW (high of week), PFL = LOW (low of week), printed on the
rules slide at burned `43:17`.

**Reasoning trace.** I searched for definitional language rather than for the term. The term
`peak formation` occurs 54 times; almost all are chart narration. The one definitional use is a
relative clause inside a digression about whether the method is "multi-timeframe analysis". I
flagged in `V10_INTERPRETATION.md` Q8 that a relative clause is thinner evidence than a printed
definition, which is why `A-010` is NARROWED rather than CLOSED.

## Q3 — Is the "blue box" the same object as the "Asian range"? What does V10 let you conclude?

**Answer.** V10 **uses them interchangeably** for the same object on the same chart — `[01:01:42]`
*"he doesn't exploit the Asian range … he doesn't break the Asian range"* describes precisely the
condition the printed slide states as *"above or below blue box"*. **But that identification does
not make either one computable**, because V10 **never states a clock time for any session** — six
candidate session times occur zero times in the body. So the correct conclusion is: *probably the
same object, still undefined.*

> **The attractive wrong answer I rejected:** *"Yes — blue box = Asian session high/low, so it's
> defined."* That is the `D-030` failure. Identifying an undefined object with another undefined
> object yields an undefined object. Recorded as `A-076`.

## Q4 — State the safety trade's preconditions in the order the lesson prints them.

**Answer.** From the slide at `43:17`, in order:
1. PFH/PFL has formed as HOW or LOW (4hr tie-in);
2. price has moved away from the area and has confirmed the formation;
3. level one consolidation is clear;
4. DNC — do not counter the breakout back towards the peak.

Then the triggers, slide `46:17`: 5. a visible stop hunt, preferably above or below the blue box;
6. the "straight-away" develops here; 7. obvious ones are still valid; 8. the dealer issues a
second leg M or W, which locks the trade; 9. if the level is hit a third time, *"God help the
dealer"*.

**Reasoning trace.** I took the order from the printed slides rather than the narration, because
the narration interleaves them with digressions. The two slides are consecutive and the spoken pass
at `[00:43:01]`–`[00:47:16]` follows the same order, so there is no conflict to resolve.

## Q5 — What is the DNC, and what does the lesson say happens to someone who ignores it?

**Answer.** **Do Not Counter** — `[01:20:59]` *"It's not the demilitarized zone. It's a do not
counter."* It forbids trading the breakout **back towards the peak formation** during level-one
consolidation. The lesson's stated consequence: you are a *"sucker"* (`[00:45:38]`, printed on the
slide), and mechanically, `[01:01:02]` *"if you counter this and he straight rises he screwed you
up."*

**Reasoning trace.** The reason given is asymmetry of information, not morality: at that point the
dealer has two continuations available (straight move or stop hunt) and countering bets against the
one you cannot rule out.

## Q6 — The safety trade's distance figure. What is it, from what, and how confident should you be?

**Answer.** **25 to 75 pips.** The number is settled by the **printed slide** at burned `75:57`:
*"Look for the stop hunt to come in around 25 to 75 pips off of the LOW/HOW anchor."* **The
reference point is NOT settled** — four of six spoken instances say *"off of the blue tracer"*,
which the lesson treats as a **different, per-day** object. Filed as `C-017`; the number is
`A-078`.

> **The attractive wrong answer I rejected:** *"25 to 75 pips off the blue tracer"*, on the grounds
> that the speaker says it four times and says the anchor version only twice. **Frequency is not
> authority.** But the converse rescue — "the slide wins under `D-008`, so it's the anchor" — is
> also refused: `D-008` ranks source classes by capture reliability, not by authorial intent
> between two things one speaker said in one hour. **The honest answer is that the number is
> settled and its origin is not.**

## Q7 — What does the safety trade use as a stop loss?

**Answer.** **Nothing. V10 states no stop loss of any kind.** The token `stop loss` occurs **zero**
times in the lesson. The trade is given an entry, a direction, a target (*"plus 50"*) and a holding
period — and no invalidation price.

**Reasoning trace.** This is an absence question and I checked it by search rather than by memory,
precisely because the plausible-sounding answer (*"beyond the peak formation high"*) is the kind of
thing a confident reader supplies. It is nowhere in the lesson. The related quarantined `RULES.md`
asserts *"Place your stop loss 10 to 15 pips beyond the High or Low of the Day"* at `[00:18:00]`,
where the actual audio is about not mailing in homework (`Q-011` §3).

## Q8 — How long does the directional lock last?

**Answer.** **The lesson gives incompatible figures**, and the honest answer is that it does not
say. `[01:00:41]` *"a one-day lock on the directional bias"*; `[01:00:43]`, two seconds later,
*"the lock is good for three days"*; `[00:41:47]` and `[01:32:07]` *"at least two days"* / *"two
days"*; `[01:26:50]` day three *"looking for the reversal, maybe one more day rise"*. Filed as
`C-016`.

**Reasoning trace.** I looked for a single authoritative statement and found five. A reconciliation
is available (the *lock* lasts three days, the *rise* two) but the speaker never says it, so
adopting it would be the composite-definition move `SOURCING_HIERARCHY.md` §3.2 forbids. This
matters more than a bookkeeping conflict because, per Q7, **time is the trade's only non-target
exit.**

## Q9 — On the lesson's own numbers, how many safety trades should a ten-pair watchlist yield per week?

**Answer.** **Twenty**, reduced to **five or six** under the stricter filter. `[01:10:01]` *"if you
make a high of the week and a low of the week, that's two trades per pair per week"*; `[00:55:37]`
ten pairs × two reversals = *"20 trades a week"*; then `[01:01:53]`–`[01:02:04]`, under the
break-the-Asian-range filter for strugglers, *"you went from 20 trades down to five or six."*

> **The attractive wrong answer I rejected:** *"two per pair per week, so 20, and the lesson says
> you'll make plus 50 on all of them with no losers."* He does say *"You're gonna make plus 50 have
> no losers"* at `[01:02:04]` — **but that is said of the FIVE OR SIX**, not of the twenty, and
> `[00:55:44]` explicitly says of the twenty that *"you may not get the second leg visible."*
> Attaching the no-losers claim to the larger number would be `E03`, a dropped qualifier.

## Q10 — What does the lesson say about the timing of entry relative to stops?

**Answer.** *"Don't trade until they hit the stops"* — stated at least seven times, printed at
burned `23:03`, given a pre-trade checklist form at `[00:25:09]` (*"Did the dealer hit the stops
before I pull the trigger?"*), and given a categorical consequence at `[01:06:49]`: *"Any other
setup without a stop hunt is a shit gamble."*

**Reasoning trace.** Its priority is stated explicitly at `[00:23:36]`: *"The pattern, the pattern,
the pattern, timing, the levels"* — timing is fourth, after pattern thrice. The lesson's own worked
failure at `[00:23:28]` is a **valid M** that lost **on timing**, which is the cleanest
discrimination example in the file: correct pattern, wrong moment.

## Q11 — Does V10 teach the TDI?

**Answer.** **No. It defers it.** `[01:13:03]`: *"do we use TDI to confirm these? Yes, that's next
week's lesson my friend."* The TDI **panel is visible** in three curated frames (`29:12`, `83:42`,
`87:17`, the last captioned `Traders Dynamic Index Visual`), so it is **displayed, not taught** —
the same disposition `A-039` already carries.

> **The attractive wrong answer I rejected:** *"Yes — it's on the charts throughout and he mentions
> the shark fin."* He does say *"Shark fin blood in the water"* at `[01:24:29]`. **But narrating a
> chart that has an indicator on it is not teaching the indicator**, and the one time he is asked
> directly, he defers. Under `D-025` consequence 4 / `D-033`, a visible panel is descriptive
> evidence that it is displayed and nothing more.

## Q12 — What session times does V10 give?

**Answer.** **None.** V10 discusses sessions constantly — `asian` occurs 13 times, plus London, US,
and the *"first eight hours of the week"* — and **never states a single clock time for any of them.**

**Reasoning trace.** Second absence question, and the one most likely to be answered from the
corpus rather than from V10: V02 has a printed session table, and the quarantined `NOTES.md` for
V10 asserts *"Asian 7:00 PM – 3:00 AM EST / London 3:30 AM – 9:00 AM EST / New York 9:30 AM – 5:00
PM EST."* I tested all six of those figures against V10's body: **six figures, six zero counts**
(`Q-011` §1). The correct answer is about **this lesson**, and this lesson gives none — which is
also why `A-076` cannot be closed.
