## THE CYCLE

**Learn → Attempt → Grade → Diagnose → Reattempt → Pass**

A lesson is not complete merely because the video ended.

## THE FIRST ATTEMPT IS PERMANENT

Preserve it even when — especially when — it is wrong. The first attempt is the
only evidence of independent understanding that exists. Everything after it is
informed by the answer.

The reviewer grades this distinction explicitly:

```text
FIRST-PASS SUCCESS          ← correct unaided
SUCCESS AFTER CORRECTION    ← corrected by self-diagnosis
SUCCESS AFTER SOURCE REVIEW ← corrected only after seeing the answer
UNRESOLVED
```

Relabelling a `SUCCESS AFTER SOURCE REVIEW` as `FIRST-PASS SUCCESS` is a
research-integrity finding, not a small error.

## ALSO RECORD

- The assignment as stated, with its timestamp.
- Reasoning, not just answers — the reasoning is what is being assessed.
- Confidence at the time of the first attempt, recorded before grading.
- Root cause of any error, and **whether the same misunderstanding contaminated
  other artifacts** produced in the same session. It usually did.
