## §1 — COMPREHENSION: THE LESSON, RESTATED WITHOUT LOOKING BACK

*Written from memory before re-reading `V15_SOURCE_NOTES.md`, then checked against it. Two errors
were found on checking and are marked ⚠ rather than corrected away.*

**The ADR is the average daily range. It is normally an oscillator in a sub-panel, and in that
form the instructor says it is worthless to him. His version plots its high and low as horizontal
lines on the price chart, so that "when these values are hit we can make solid trade decisions".**

**The levels move during the day** — they "creep up or creep down to try to fit price action" — so
he calls them a **floating grid**, and contrasts them with **pivots**, which are a **fixed grid**.
When the floating grid drifts onto the fixed grid and they line up, that coincidence is the thing
to look for.

**Its main use is the New York reversal.** If a pair has **met or exceeded** its ADR, it goes on
the radar and the pairs that have not are dropped from the screen. That is a **watchlist filter**,
not an entry.

**The entry comes from confluence**, and the printed rule names four objects: *yesterday's high and
low (the "blue tracer"), a pivot point, the ADR marker*, and — "especially" — *a longer-period
moving average, the 50 or the 200*. Spoken, he adds the **TDI shark fin / blood in the water**, and
a time: around **9:30**, because "the new market maker comes on" at the session change and
**"don't trade until they hit the stops"**.

⚠ **Error 1, found on checking:** I had remembered the ADR as *"about 150 pips"*. The lesson says
**"about 200 pips"** `[00:27:39]`; **150** is the Tier-2 PDF's worked example. **Two sources, two
numbers, and I had merged them** — which is precisely the failure `A-095` is written to prevent.

⚠ **Error 2, found on checking:** I had remembered *"M1/M3 day"* as being explained in the lesson.
It is **not**: `[00:45:47]` says *"This is an M1, M3 day. I'll talk about that next, if you're
confused"* — **and he does not.** The explanation I remembered is the **Tier-2 PDF's**
(`A-096`). **A promise to explain is not an explanation**, and misremembering one as the other is
how Tier 2 quietly becomes Tier 1.
