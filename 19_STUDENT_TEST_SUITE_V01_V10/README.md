# Videos 1–10 Student Mastery Test Suite

## Purpose

This directory is a standalone, manual assessment of what a student can correctly recall, recognise, reject, sequence, calculate, mark up, source, and leave unresolved after Videos 1–10. It tests rule application, not profitability. It does not begin or use Video 11.

## Governing boundary

- Tier 1 course evidence is the Videos 1–10 recordings, their verified screenshots, and reliable transcripts.
- Instructor homework/solutions follow Tier 1; student source notes and interpretations are lower tiers.
- The admitted seminar notes are Tier 2 and are always labelled `MMM-NOTES`; they cannot override a recording.
- Owner attestations and project interpretations are never presented as direct course teaching.
- Every answer is labelled `EXPLICIT`, `VISUAL`, `IMPLIED`, `INFERRED`, or `UNRESOLVED`.
- Development-period historical evidence may support or contradict a claim, but it does not validate a trading edge.
- The `2016-07-01 → 2017-12-29` holdout is not used or opened.

## Formal status warning

Videos 1–8 have independent reviewer `PASS` records. V09 and V10 are marked complete only through owner-directed self-verification; neither has the final independent pass its normal review lineage called for. The last arm's-length V10 decision is R1 `REVISE` with 0 critical, 0 major, and 4 minor findings. This suite therefore treats V09/V10 content as studied and remediated, but not normally reviewer-certified.

## Administration

1. Assign an attempt ID and copy `RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md` before opening the packet.
2. Disable access to `INSTRUCTOR_ANSWER_KEY.md` and all answer-key notes.
3. For chart cases, show only the named image/CSV and stop replay at the exact decision timestamp. Do not expose later candles.
4. Preserve the student's first classification, markup, calculation, reasoning, and confidence before any reveal or feedback.
5. Require a provenance label and uncertainty statement on every case.
6. Grade the process before considering any later outcome. A valid setup may lose; an invalid decision may win.
7. Apply critical-failure rules before computing the aggregate score.

## Non-goals

This suite is not a master trading specification, entry signal service, strategy, Pine Script, optimisation exercise, proof of profitability, or substitute for independent review. Undefined concepts remain undefined.
