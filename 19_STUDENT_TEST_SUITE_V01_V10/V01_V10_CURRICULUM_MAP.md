## Curriculum gaps that control assessment design

1. A complete, prospectively testable chart-entry rule does not yet exist across Videos 1–10 because several recognition inputs are undefined and V10 defers TDI to V11.
2. Retrospective weekly extremes are measurable; a real-time peak/anchor trigger is not fully specified.
3. Risk sizing is explicit only after a valid stop distance is known; V10 gives none for the safety trade.
4. Guest and instructor accounts are both Tier 1 after D-033, so disagreement must be preserved, not silently blended.
5. Historical results are mixed and development-only; they teach evidence calibration, not profitability.
6. The empty concept library and chart-example folders prevent claims of complete blind recognition mastery.
