Study these distinctions before answering the separate retest cases.

## 22 / 2-2

The course definition retained by the project is **the second leg of a second leg at a repeated
level**. A slight overshoot may remain recognizable when price returns or closes through the
repeated level. This does not establish objective leg anatomy, segmentation, the meaning of
`slight`, invalidation, or a complete entry.

- Separately admitted `MMM-NOTES` p.64: **three levels over three days plus an intraday Level III**,
  treated there as a strong sell/reversal signal.

## PFH/PFL plus M/W

PFH+M is a bearish/short candidate at a completed weekly high. PFL+W is a bullish/long candidate at
a completed weekly low. During a live week, use candidate/high-low-so-far; final extremes and
complete M/W anatomy are not prospectively established.

## Safety Trade

The seven printed points are:

1. visible trap yesterday / PFL low;
2. handles breakout and 200 traders;
3. 25–75 pips off yesterday's low of day;
4. cuts the Asian range as a visible stop hunt;
5. spike and comes above for one hour;
6. W–TDI blood;
7. consolidation take-profit.

Only points 3 and 4 are presently measurable under declared conventions. The other five depend on
undefined peak/trap handling, spike/clock start, W/TDI, consolidation or exit rules.

## Retrieval boundary

Known setup names and roles must be returned even when their entry definitions are incomplete.
Machine-codability is a separate status. Never turn a named setup into a complete live trade rule
when its anatomy, timing, invalidation or risk inputs remain unresolved.
