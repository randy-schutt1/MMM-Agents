# Chart-Heavy Practical Examination — Videos 1–10

## Purpose and boundary

This is a separate, chart-first assessment for the AI student. It does not replace the 88-case curriculum suite or alter any prior attempt. It assesses whether the student can mark, measure, reject, sequence, calculate, and explain Videos 1–10 concepts on charts without hindsight.

The exam deliberately does **not** begin Video 11, manufacture missing TDI rules, create a master strategy, claim profitability, or convert development-period results into a validated edge.

## Exam composition

- 60 scored cases, 12 points each, 720 points total.
- 52 new historical decision-chart assets from distinct GBP/USD development weeks not used by the earlier suite.
- 8 unaltered original Bootcamp chart screenshots used for source/provenance audits.
- 52 matching visible-only CSV slices.
- 4 sealed 24-hour continuation charts for outcome-versus-application checks.
- Every scored case requires chart markup or chart-source annotation.

## Administration

1. Give the student only `STUDENT_PRACTICAL_PACKET.md`, `RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md`, and `assets/`.
2. Do not expose `INSTRUCTOR_ANSWER_KEY.md`, `instructor_only/`, `COVERAGE_MATRIX.md`, this README's answer-adjacent materials, or any prior answers.
3. Assign cases in packet order. The student must duplicate the named image, mark the duplicate, provide its path/hash, answer, and lock the response.
4. For `A01`–`A04`, do not reveal the continuation image until Phase A is immutable.
5. Grade rule application before observing or discussing outcome.
6. Preserve the first attempt. Corrections and retests are appended as separate records.

## Source discipline

Recording evidence controls doctrine. Original screenshot annotations establish what was visibly demonstrated, not a universal mechanical rule. Generated historical charts establish observable OHLC facts only. Calculations derived from those facts are `INFERRED`. Undefined recognition inputs remain `UNRESOLVED`.

The blue first-eight-hours band is an administration aid grounded in the V03 exercise; it is not asserted to be a universally defined Bootcamp blue box.
