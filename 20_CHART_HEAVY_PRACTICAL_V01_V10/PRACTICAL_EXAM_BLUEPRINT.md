# Practical Examination Blueprint

## Design rationale

Videos 1–10 contain many chart teachings but do not yet provide a complete prospectively mechanical entry specification. The practical therefore maximizes real chart work while grading only traceable claims:

- exact OHLC measurements and first-eight-hours markup;
- disciplined application of explicit prohibitions and sequences;
- retrospective PFH/PFL identification on completed weeks;
- basic confirmation when context and reversal identity are stipulated;
- risk calculations when a valid stop input is separately stipulated;
- refusal to fabricate level, box, push, stop-hunt, M/W, second-leg, TDI, or prospective peak definitions;
- auditing what original recording screenshots visibly demonstrate.

## Blocks

| Block | Cases | Chart task | Primary emphasis |
|---|---:|---|---|
| A | 12 | New visible-only weekly opening charts | First eight hours, first breach, V01 first-move discipline |
| B | 8 | New Tuesday hard-right-edge charts | High/low-so-far versus final PFH/PFL |
| C | 8 | New completed-week charts | PFH/PFL and exact pip-range calculation |
| D | 8 | New raw decision charts | Lookalikes, insufficient evidence, undefined geometry, reasons to stay out |
| E | 8 | New labelled-candle charts | Reversal/direction-candle sequence and no anticipation |
| F | 8 | New chart-anchored risk plans | 2% cumulative exposure, calculation, recalculation, missing-stop boundary |
| G | 8 | Original Bootcamp chart screenshots | Visual doctrine, source provenance, contamination and transfer limits |

## Balance

- 18 positive/valid cases.
- 18 negative/lookalike cases.
- 16 borderline/insufficient-evidence cases.
- 8 neutral provenance audits.

Each case is worth 12 points. All 60 require direct chart markup, chart measurement, or annotation of an original source chart. Four A-block cases add staged reveal checks but do not award outcome-based validity points.

## Practical competencies

The exam directly samples chart markup, recognition, discrimination, sequence, calculation, provenance, ambiguity calibration, contradiction awareness, and hindsight control. Recall is embedded in applied chart tasks rather than asked as isolated vocabulary.

Risk appears as chart-anchored planning rather than as evidence that a raw chart is a valid setup. The V09 formula is graded only after a stop distance is stipulated independently. V10's missing safety-trade stop is itself tested.

## Retest policy

Retests use different unused development weeks and new account inputs. A chart contaminated by future data is replaced in full. Ambiguity failures use new raw charts and mixed source cards. Risk failures use fresh multi-position allocations. The original attempt and marked charts remain immutable.
