## Status meaning

`CLOSED — REVIEWER REMEDIATED AT OWNER DIRECTION` means:

- the lesson was independently reviewed from source;
- the reviewer found and corrected the recorded issues at the owner's direction;
- the original `REVISE` record is preserved;
- no third-session review is required; and
- the lesson is complete and eligible for cumulative/final review.

promote any setup to machine-codable or any development result to a trading edge.
