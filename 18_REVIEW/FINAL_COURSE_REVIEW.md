## 9. THE RECONSTRUCTION TEST

> If the original instructor and source videos disappeared, could a competent trader reconstruct
> the methodology from this repository alone?

**Answer: PARTIALLY — therefore not sufficient.**

The trader could reconstruct the vocabulary, source-tiered setup inventory, broad weekly/session
narrative, several partial sequences, risk arithmetic, warnings, and the exact boundaries of what
remains unknown. The trader could not reconstruct a consistent raw-chart method for deciding:

1. where the first leg of an M or W begins and ends;
2. how raw price is segmented into pushes and Levels 1–3;
3. how PFH/PFL is identified prospectively rather than retrospectively;
4. the blue tracer, TDI, ADR and pivot inputs needed by later confluence teaching;
5. one complete setup's entry, invalidation, stop, management and exit path;
6. how the full method performs on unseen charts without outcome information.

Because the governing standard requires `YES`, `PARTIALLY` is blocking. A polished partial Master
Specification would hide these holes behind authoritative prose.

## THE ULTIMATE STANDARD

> Would I be comfortable allowing future code, automated backtests, and eventually real-money
> execution to depend on this corpus?

**No.** I would allow targeted research and source acquisition to depend on the corpus because it
preserves evidence and uncertainty unusually well. I would not allow automated execution to depend
on it because the missing decisions occur before entry, where a system must choose direction,
recognize structure and avoid hindsight.

The correct resolution is to preserve the 21-video study, use the gap matrix as the next research
agenda, and withhold Phase 3 until the method can be reconstructed without supplying its central
facts by hand.

**Evidence before confidence. Understanding before automation. Validation before optimization.
Capital protection before execution.**
