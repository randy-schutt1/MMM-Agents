# Test Blueprint

## Difficulty design

| Tier | Cases | Decision horizon | Signature evidence | Practical challenge |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Easy | E01–E12 | Monday trading-day close | Complete or single-condition stipulations | Large/prominent historical extrema; direct checklist application |
| Intermediate | I01–I12 | Tuesday trading-day close | Scrambled, incomplete, conflicting, or separately named setups | More candles, competing extremes, sequence and taxonomy separation |
| Difficult | D01–D12 | Wednesday trading-day close | Raw OHLC only | Dense chart, close competing extrema, correct refusal to certify undefined signature geometry |
| Mixed direction | M01–M10 | Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday close | Complete, invalid, incomplete, conflicting, or raw | Commit to BUY/SELL/NO TRADE/WAIT/UNRESOLVED and mark the decision sequence |

The historical weeks were ranked by extrema prominence. More visually prominent weeks were assigned to the easy tier; close competing extremes were assigned to the difficult tier.

## Required response sequence

### Phase A

1. Confirm the printed 17:00–16:45 target-day window.
2. Locate maximum wick high and minimum wick low inside that window.
3. Mark HOD/LOD with exact prices and timestamps.
4. Inventory the supplied safety-trade prerequisites.
5. Apply direction-away/DNC logic.
6. Check visible stop hunt and second-leg conditions.
7. Classify the official signature checklist.
8. Identify a 22 or straightaway only when separately stipulated.
9. Lock the answer and marked-chart hash.

For an `M` case, also mark the hard-right-edge decision candle, directional arrow, and the precise prerequisite or invalidation controlling BUY, SELL, NO TRADE, WAIT, or UNRESOLVED.

### Phase B

1. Verify Phase A is immutable.
2. Reveal the completed week.
3. Locate the week's maximum wick high and minimum wick low.
4. Mark HOW/LOW with exact prices and timestamps.
5. Preserve Phase A signature classification verbatim.
6. Lock the completed-week markup and hash.

## Evidence design

- Extrema are `VISUAL`; extracting exact values from the matching CSV is `INFERRED` measurement.
- Safety-trade designation and checklist are `EXPLICIT` V10 doctrine.
- Positive signature cases rely on explicitly printed case stipulations, not inferred raw-chart anatomy.
- Raw-chart certification gaps are `UNRESOLVED`.
- Completed-week movement is never evidence that Phase A was correct or incorrect.
