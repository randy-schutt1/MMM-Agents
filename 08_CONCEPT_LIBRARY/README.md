## RULES

1. **Use the instructor's terminology.** Renaming a concept to a familiar term from
   another framework is terminology drift (E16).
2. **Do not finalize the ontology early.** Let the course reveal the hierarchy — a
   concept that looks fundamental at V02 may be a special case of something taught
   at V11.
3. **No concept without evidence.** Every entry cites video and timestamp, and
   classifies each element `EXPLICIT` / `VISUAL` / `IMPLIED` / `INFERRED` /
   `UNRESOLVED`.
4. **No numeric thresholds during the Student Phase.** Those go to
   `10_AMBIGUITIES/` as `DO NOT CODE`.
5. **Revisions are appended, never overwritten.** Concepts legitimately evolve as
   later lessons refine them, and the cumulative review needs to see how.
6. **Misidentifications matter as much as definitions.** An entry with only
   positive examples has demonstrated familiarity, not discrimination.
