## RULES

1. **Use the instructor's terminology.** Renaming a concept to a more familiar term
   from another framework is terminology drift (E16).
2. **Do not finalize the ontology early.** Let the course reveal the hierarchy.
   A concept that looks fundamental at V02 may turn out to be a special case of
   something taught at V11.
3. **One file per concept**, registered here.
4. **Revisions are appended**, never overwritten — concepts legitimately evolve, and
   the cumulative review needs to see how.
5. **No concept without evidence.** Every entry cites video and timestamp.
6. **No numeric thresholds** during the Student Phase. Those belong in the ambiguity
   log as `DO NOT CODE`.
