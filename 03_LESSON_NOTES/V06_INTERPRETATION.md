## 1. WORKING UNDERSTANDING

*(My reading of what the lesson claims. Not doctrine. Every clause is guest material.)*

The presenter's claim, reduced to its skeleton, is a **self-similarity argument with an
operational payload**:

1. The market-maker cycle repeats at every timeframe — 4h, 1h, 15m — so the same three-part
   shape the course teaches on higher timeframes should be visible inside a single day.
   `EXPLICIT` `[00:00:17]`–`[00:00:46]`
2. The 15-minute instance of it is renamed **pushes**, explicitly to avoid colliding with the
   instructor's word *levels*. `EXPLICIT` `[00:01:28]`–`[00:01:41]`
3. Pushes begin at the **anchor**, which is the same object the course already uses; the count
   starts at the **second leg**. `EXPLICIT` `[00:21:55]`, `[01:10:04]`
4. Each push is roughly **ADR ÷ 3**, separated by pullbacks of **25–50 pips**, and each turn is
   marked by a **bounce or trap at a moving average** that forms one of **three named candle
   patterns**. `EXPLICIT` `[00:05:45]`, `[00:06:04]`, `[00:07:09]`–`[00:07:34]`
5. You enter only **after rejection**, only on a **nameable** pattern, only **in the direction
   of the market-maker trend**, and you exit on a **contrary pattern** or after **two hours**
   of nothing. `EXPLICIT` `[00:08:06]`, `[00:08:00]`, `[00:18:25]`, `[00:40:26]`, `[00:13:19]`
6. When you are counter-trend, the structure degrades: you get two pushes, not three. This is
   offered as the *diagnostic* that you are counter-trend. `EXPLICIT` `[00:37:29]`–`[00:37:47]`

**What I think the lesson is actually for, as distinct from what it says.** `INFERRED`

The stated benefit is more trades: *"You're taking three, four trades in one trend run in that
one day compared to the way Steve and a couple of other people trade"* `[00:44:55]`. But the
lesson's most repeated content is **not** how to take those trades — it is a **list of reasons
not to**: no touch of the moving averages → no count `[00:20:57]`, no entry `[00:21:43]`;
close-but-not-touching → pass `[00:24:13]`; not nameable → *"garbage"* `[00:27:56]`; anticipating
→ *"Don't guess"* `[00:36:08]`; counter-trend → *"they're not clear"* `[00:07:03]`.

My reading is that **the lesson is a frequency upgrade sold with a filter attached, and the
filter is the substance.** That reading is `INFERRED` and I hold it loosely; see §6.

## 5. TENSIONS AND WHAT I DID NOT RESOLVE

### 5.1 The lesson's own numbers do not reconcile with its own claims `UNRESOLVED`

The lesson states *"Each push is approximately ADR divided by 3"* `[00:05:45]` and *"The pull marks
[pullbacks] are usually 25 to 50 pips"* `[00:06:04]` — the slide prints the second as *"The
pullbacks are usually 25 to 50 pips"* (frame `V06_00-05-29`). On the majors of that era an ADR of ~120–150 pips would
put a push at ~40–50 pips — **the same size as the pullbacks that are supposed to separate
them.** A structure whose advances and retracements are the same size is not obviously a
three-push trend.

I have **not** resolved this and deliberately have not tried to. Resolving it would require
choosing an ADR value and a lookback, which is `D-030`'s prohibition exactly: the course has not
defined ADR (`A-038`), so any number I picked would be mine. **The tension is recorded as an
observation about the lesson's internal consistency, not as a finding against it.**
