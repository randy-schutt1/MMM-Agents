## 1. WORKING UNDERSTANDING

**Confidence for this summary as a whole: MEDIUM.**

That is one grade higher than V01, and the reason is worth stating: V02 is much more
densely taught. Roughly 41 of its 60 minutes are substantive against V01's 21, it
works through named chart examples continuously, and it states two homework
assignments precisely. It is also the lesson where the course's central analogy is
made explicit.

My reading of what V02 teaches:

**The weekly chart is the intraday pattern, drawn larger.** This is the load-bearing
idea. The instructor maps his intraday cycle onto the five-day week one element at a
time: Sunday and Monday are "the Asian session for the week" and therefore
accumulation; the dealer's aggressive move out of that range is the weekly equivalent
of the intraday stop hunt; the midweek reversal is the anchor point; and the rest of
the week is a unidirectional run away from it. He says this in as many words at
`[00:14:08]` — "the same structure that I've illustrated for you is the same
structure as the same structure" — and demonstrates it against charts for twenty
minutes. If V01 established that a weekly cycle exists, V02 is the lesson that says
*why it has the shape it has*.

**Consequences he draws from that mapping, in his order:**

Once the anchor point is in, the direction is known for 2.5–4 days and the extent is
projected with ADR × 3 — three *cycles*, he insists, not three days. The dealer will
hold price beyond the trapped traders' level for that whole period, deliberately, to
exhaust them; he will not release them by crossing back. The first sign the run is
over is an "outside structure high", after which exactly two things can happen —
half-Batman to the low, or finish the M formation in London and correct.

**What he asks the student to actually do** is narrower than what he explains. Three
behavioural instructions carry almost all the actionable weight: take only second-leg
trades; take profit at session changeover using a limit; and scratch out at −15 rather
than take heat. Everything else in the hour is diagnosis, not execution.

**Where my confidence drops sharply** is at the point where the lesson would have to
become tradeable. "Second leg" is the pivot on which all three behavioural
instructions rest, and V02 promises to define it — "And I'm going to define what a
second leg is" `[00:35:22]` — and then defines it only by pointing at a chart. That
is not a transcription failure; it is what happened. See §9.

## 9. WHAT I AM NOT CONFIDENT ABOUT

Stated plainly, because the mastery report depends on it.

**The lesson does not contain a complete testable rule, and the gap is in the same
place as V01's.**

V02 is a substantially better lesson than V01 — more teaching, more examples, real
homework, and a genuinely clarifying central analogy. It also states more numbers
than V01 did. But the numbers cluster on the *management* side (when to take profit,
when to scratch) and the *projection* side (ADR × 3), and the entry side stays empty.
Specifically:

1. **Entry trigger: absent.** Every entry instruction routes through "second leg",
   which the lesson promises to define and then defines only by gesture.
2. **Entry filter: deferred.** The trading zone, which V01 named as the entry filter,
   is deferred again — now to V03.
3. **Stop loss: explicitly deferred.** "Probably not today."
4. **Position size: absent.**
5. **Target: partially present but ungrounded.** ADR × 3 is a real formula, but
   "cycle" is undefined and no ADR lookback is stated, so it cannot be computed.

So the honest position is: V02 supplies **exit and management parameters for a trade
whose entry the course has not yet specified.** That is a coherent thing for lesson 2
of 21 to do, and it is not a defect in the lesson. It does mean V02 cannot pass a
mastery standard that requires a complete testable rule, and it must not be made to
pass by borrowing V01's material or the quarantined files' inventions.

**Specific low-confidence items:**

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Anything depending on "second leg" (I22, I24, I25, M5) | The term is undefined and load-bearing |
| Any day count (I15, M7, M8) | C-001 unresolved and now twice-witnessed |
| The two-branch claim at outside structure high (I18) | Both branches contain undefined terms |
| Any session time (I21, I29, I30, M3, M6) | No timezone; instructor explicitly declines to reason about DST |
| "Mayonnaise" as a decision input (§6) | The referent is unknown |
| The letter sequence for "the cycle" (Q4) | Two incompatible ASR readings |

**What I am confident about:** the weekly/intraday structural analogy is what this
lesson teaches, it is stated repeatedly and unambiguously, and it is the correct thing
to carry forward. The homework is unambiguous and fully recoverable. The three
behavioural instructions (second-leg-only, limit at changeover, scratch at −15) are
what he actually asks for, in his own words, and only the first is unusable.
