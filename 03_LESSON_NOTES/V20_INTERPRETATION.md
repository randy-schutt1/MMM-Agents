# V20 — INTERPRETATION

**What this file is:** the student session's reading of V20, with confidence graded per claim.
**What it is not:** evidence. Every claim traces to `V20_SOURCE_NOTES.md`, which traces to the
marker grid in `02_TRANSCRIPTS/V20/V20_TRANSCRIPT.md`.

**Confidence scale:** `CERTAIN` — stated explicitly and unambiguously, corroborated.
`HIGH` — stated explicitly, single source, no contradiction. `MEDIUM` — stated but qualified, or
requires one inference step. `LOW` — inferred; the lesson does not say it. `BLOCKED` — cannot be
evaluated without something the course has not supplied (`D-030`).

---

## §1 — THE ONE-SENTENCE READING

**V20 argues that the single most important thing on a chart is the aggressive one-legged move that
made the extreme — the *outside structure* — and that everything else in the method is a way of
trading off it.** The M, the W, the Half-A-Batman, the spike and the railroad track are all
described here as **parts or consequences of that one move**, and the lesson closes by naming what
must follow it: **Level 3 chop**, then a resolution away from the level.

That reading is the lesson's own: `[00:00:00]` *"All trap moves will come in as an outside
structure"* and `[00:43:02]` *"The outside structure is the first formation of this."*
**CONFIDENCE: HIGH.** The two sentences are explicit; calling it the lesson's organising principle
is the interpretive step.

## §2 — THE CLAIMS, GRADED

### 2.1 The outside structure

> The aggressive **single-leg** move that sets the high or low of the day — and often of the week —
> is the *outside structure*. It is the **vector portion** of a Half-A-Batman (and, spoken but not
> printed, of an M or W).

**CONFIDENCE that this is taught: CERTAIN.** Defined in speech `[00:00:00]`–`[00:01:05]`, printed on
the `00:25` slide in five clauses, given a printed title slide at `00:17`, and worked through six
charts.

⚠️ **CONFIDENCE that it is the vector of an M/W: MEDIUM, and this is a deliberate downgrade.**
`[00:00:10]` and `[00:00:28]` both say it. **The printed slide says only *"The Vector portion of The
Half-A- Batman"*.** `04_SCREENSHOTS/V20/INDEX.md` §2 records the divergence. **The audio is the only
source for the M/W half of the claim, and this project has spent nine lessons learning that the deck
usually settles the audio rather than the other way round.**

⚠️ **`[00:01:41]`'s *"absolute sign of reversal"* is NOT graded as a rule.** It is stated twice and
it is an **unqualified universal** — the kind of sentence `REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md` §6.D's failure chain
is about. **The lesson supplies no counterexample, no failure rate and no invalidation condition for
it.** Recorded as **taught**, not as **established**.

### 2.2 ⭐⭐ The 30-minute time cap — V20 IS THE THIRD WITNESS AND IT SAYS `30`, FLAT

> After the previous high is broken, price must close back below it **in 30 minutes or less**. If
> the dealer **stays above**, scratch the trade — he is about to extend to the next strike zone.

**CONFIDENCE that this is taught: CERTAIN.** `[00:27:44]`–`[00:28:35]`, and it is the same rule V19
states at `[00:22:43]` and writes onto its `M` slide as `with in 30M`.

⭐⭐ **WHAT V20 ADDS, AND IT IS THE THING `COURSE_PROGRESS.md`'s CARRY-FORWARD (c) ASKED FOR:**

1. **A student asks whether 15 minutes is enough and is told no** — `[00:28:01]` *"In 15 minutes, do
   I get out? **No.**"* **The cap has a floor as well as a ceiling.**
2. ⭐ **An ACTION on failure, which V19 never gave:** *"If the dealer stays above the previous high
   […] **I recommend you scratch the trade out**"* `[00:28:09]`.
3. ⭐ **A close-quality qualifier:** the re-entry below must be *"**on a nice solid close**"*
   `[00:28:27]`.

⚠️⚠️ **AND WHAT IT DOES *NOT* DO IS SETTLE `C-029`.** V19's deck contradicts itself on camera —
`30M` on the `M` slide, `30 to 45 m` on the `W` slide. **V20 says `30 minutes or less` and never
says `45`, `90` or `30 to 45` at all.** ⛔ **But V20's passage is explicitly the M-side case**: the
student is *"shorting here"* `[00:28:01]` and the level is *"the previous **high**"*. **So V20 is a
third witness to the `M` slide's figure and is SILENT on the `W` slide's.** **`C-029` narrows; it
does not close.** Recorded as `C-030`.

### 2.3 ⭐⭐ The one-third entry — the most mechanically complete entry rule in the corpus

> Measure the **handle**. Divide by three. Place the entry **one third off the high** of it.

**CONFIDENCE that this is taught: CERTAIN.** `[00:29:14]`–`[00:29:53]`, introduced as *"the best way
to decide the entry"*, with a worked example in round numbers (100 pips → 33 pips off the high).

⚠️⚠️ **CONFIDENCE that it can be implemented: BLOCKED — `D-030`.** ***"Handle"* is never defined.**
It is used four times as a noun and the 100-pip example fixes only its order of magnitude. **A
one-third retracement is arithmetic; a one-third retracement *of what* is the entire rule**, and
substituting a plausible reading is exactly what `D-030` forbids. **`A-136`.**

⭐ **This is nevertheless the closest the course has come to a mechanical entry**, and it is worth
recording why the block is narrower than usual: it needs **no** moving average, **no** colour,
**no** indicator and **no** nickname. **One definition clears it.** ⚠️ **`A-136` is now the cheapest
high-value blocker in the project, and `A-133` (*blue tracer*) is the most expensive.**

### 2.4 The spike

> An aggressive change in price, usually on news, used to clear stops or move the trading zone. Its
> **direction** is set by dealer open volume. **Never enter on one.**

**CONFIDENCE: CERTAIN** — spoken `[00:20:23]`–`[00:20:51]` and printed at `20:30`, with an explicit
refusal to the direct question at `[00:26:06]` (*"Is there a way to [use a] spike for an entry? **No,
avoid it.**"*).

⚠️ **CONFIDENCE on the direction rule: BLOCKED.** *"Direction is based solely upon dealer open
volume"* is printed, and **no source, feed, proxy or threshold for open volume is given anywhere**.
**`A-137`.** ⭐ **This is a rare case where the course states a MECHANISM and withholds the
OBSERVABLE** — the rule is not vague, it is unmeasurable with what the student has.

### 2.5 Level 3

> After a powerful or extended move, price **always** enters Level 3: separated averages, chop that
> goes nowhere, and an established extreme repeatedly approached and not broken.

**CONFIDENCE that this is taught: CERTAIN.** Defined twice in speech and printed on the `39:45`
`REMEMBER` slide as the ninth bullet.

⚠️ **CONFIDENCE that it is usable: BLOCKED.** No MA periods, no separation distance, no chop
duration, no *"repeatedly"* count. **`A-138`.** ⚠️ **And *"always"* is doing heavy lifting** — the
printed bullet says *"A Powerful Or Extended Move Is **Always** Followed By Consolidation"*, which
is an unfalsifiable claim as stated unless *"powerful"* and *"consolidation"* are measurable, and
neither is.

### 2.6 ⭐⭐ The clock reference — `A-019`'s first named timezone, and what it does NOT do

> `[00:34:05]` *"I can tell you around **three thirty, four o'clock New York time**."*

**CONFIDENCE that a named timezone is finally spoken: CERTAIN.** ⭐ **This is the first clock time in
the studied corpus carrying an explicit zone**, and `A-019` has been open since V01.

⛔⛔ **AND IT DOES NOT CLOSE `A-019`, FOR A REASON WORTH STATING PRECISELY.** `A-019` asks what
timezone **the session boundaries** are defined in — the Asian box, London, the 5 pm reset.
`[00:34:05]` timestamps **the moment the instructor can read the dealer's intent**, not a session
boundary. The sentence is *"if the dealer comes out of the Asian range and [vectors] to the high
side and draws an M, then I can tell you he's going to go short for London"* — **so `3:30–4:00 NY`
is when the Asian range has resolved enough to read, which is EVIDENCE ABOUT the boundary and not a
statement OF it.**

⭐ **What it does establish, and this is worth having: the instructor's own working clock is NEW
YORK.** ⚠️ **Whether that is `EST` or `EDT` is not said, and 2012-05-20 is inside US daylight
time** — which is exactly the `D-031` Arm A / Arm B distinction. **`A-019` is EXTENDED, not closed.**
**No `PT-` in this session conditions on a named session.**

### 2.9 The trend's shape — ⭐ the most testable numeric claim V20 makes

> `39:45`, printed: *"MMs Need To Book Profit Also So The Trend Move Will Contain **20 To 25 Pip
> Pullbacks And 3 Levels Of Move With Corresponding Levels Of Consolidation**"*, and *"The Trend Move
> Is Slow And Steady"*.

**CONFIDENCE that it is taught: CERTAIN** — printed, and spoken at `[00:41:43]`–`[00:41:56]`.

and *"slow and steady"* is not a measurable. **The pullback magnitude is the only part a test can
reach without inventing a definition** — see `PT-048` §2.
