# V19 — INTERPRETATION

**What this file is:** the student session's reading of V19, with confidence graded per claim.
**What it is not:** evidence. Every claim traces to `V19_SOURCE_NOTES.md`, which traces to the
marker grid in `02_TRANSCRIPTS/V19/V19_TRANSCRIPT.md`.

**Confidence scale:** `CERTAIN` — stated explicitly and unambiguously, corroborated.
`HIGH` — stated explicitly, single source, no contradiction. `MEDIUM` — stated but qualified,
or requires one inference step. `LOW` — inferred; the lesson does not say it.
`BLOCKED` — cannot be evaluated without something the course has not yet supplied (`D-030`).

---

## §1 — THE ONE-SENTENCE READING

**V19 argues that the high and the low of the day are *manufactured*, that the manufacturing leaves
a countable signature, and that the signature has a clock on it.** The three structures it teaches —
M, W, Half-A-Batman — are not three patterns but three outcomes of one question: *did the dealer get
the volume he needed on the first move?*

That reading is the lesson's own, at `[00:50:46]`: the Half-A-Batman is *the exception* to the M/W
rule *"because when he made the trap move … he got what he was seeking so he doesn't have to come
back and trigger anything."* **CONFIDENCE: HIGH.** The unifying sentence is explicit; calling it the
lesson's organising principle is the interpretive step.

## §2 — THE CLAIMS, GRADED

### 2.1 The time cap on the second leg

> A second leg may exceed the first, but must **close back beyond it within 30 minutes** (45 at the
> outside). If it does not, the level extends to the next strike zone.

**CONFIDENCE: CERTAIN** *that the rule is taught*. It is stated at `[00:22:43]`, restated at
`[00:23:39]`, `[00:25:53]`, `[00:26:12]`, `[00:38:23]` and `[00:40:43]`, mirrored for the W at
`[00:46:38]` and `[00:47:08]`, confirmed verbatim by an independent ASR engine, **and written into
the deck on camera at `23:25` and `50:34`**.

⚠ **CONFIDENCE on the NUMBER: MEDIUM.** The lesson gives `30`, `30–45`, `45 tops`, and `30 minutes
to an hour`, and the two on-camera slide edits disagree with each other (`30M` on the M slide,
`30 to 45 m` on the W slide). `[00:26:12]` ranks them — *"30 minutes is a good rule"* — and
`[00:47:21]` supplies the only hard exclusion: *"90 minutes is too long"*. **The defensible reading
is: 30 minutes is the working figure, 45 is the tolerance, beyond 45 the setup is void.** That is an
interpretation of a spread, not a transcription. `A-134`.

### 2.2 The consequence of failing the cap

> If the dealer holds beyond the level past the cap, price extends **25 to 50 pips** to the next
> strike zone.

**CONFIDENCE: HIGH.** Four statements, all `25 to 50` once the two committed `55`s are corrected by
an independent engine and by the committed file's own later readings. ⚠ The instructor immediately
qualifies it — `[00:40:53]` *"Is it hard and fast? No, he could correct"* — so it is a tendency he
teaches, not an invariant, and the `90%` at `[00:25:34]` is **not** treated as a measurement
(`D-009`).

### 2.3 The eight-bar minimum, and the timeframe it implies

> A valid M or W consists of **at least eight bars**, counted from the bar that sets the high or low.
> Eight bars is **two hours**, so the bar is a **15-minute** bar.

**CONFIDENCE on the eight-bar minimum: CERTAIN.** Five statements (`[00:27:45]`, `[00:27:48]`,
`[00:29:13]`, `[00:41:32]`, `[00:41:52]`), plus the counting convention at `[00:32:59]`.

**CONFIDENCE on the 15-minute timeframe: HIGH — and it is DERIVED, not stated.** `[00:41:32]` gives
*"eight candles or greater … which is by the way two hours"*, and `120 ÷ 8 = 15`. **The lesson never
says "M15" — the string does not occur.** The derivation uses only the course's own two numbers, but
it is a derivation and is graded as one.

⚠⚠ **The `high 80 percent` hit rate at `[00:28:45]` is NOT graded, because it is not the course's
measurement.** It is the course reporting one student's manual count over an unstated period, with
no definition of *"successful"*, no sample size and no holdout. **It is recorded and not used.**
`D-009` and `BACKTEST_EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md` both bite here.

### 2.4 The apex depth equals the retail stop distance

> The apex of an M/W runs **15 to 25 pips** (up to 50) from the extreme, and that is **where retail
> stops sit** — 7 to 10 pips below the entry bar.

**CONFIDENCE: HIGH** that both figures are stated (`[00:42:28]`–`[00:43:04]`).
**CONFIDENCE that the equality is the mechanism: MEDIUM.** `[00:43:09]` says the dealer *"will
correct inside the formation at the apex"* to reach those stops, which supports the causal reading,
but the lesson never says *"the apex is that deep because the stops are there"* in one sentence.
**It is the obvious reading and it is an inference.**

### 2.5 Half-A-Batman

> A **Half-A-Batman** is a spike that sets the session's high or low, followed by consolidation
> **15 to 25 pips** off it for **30 to 90 minutes (up to two hours)**, after which the dealer shifts
> the zone **without returning to the level**.

**CONFIDENCE: HIGH.** Defined in speech at `[00:13:10]`–`[00:13:57]`, defined again in print at
`13:15`, and the four printed clauses agree with the spoken ones. The distinguishing test is
explicit at `[00:17:20]`: *"The dealer never comes back to the high — that is the definition."*

⚠ **CONFIDENCE on the `one hour 15 minutes` at `[00:17:11]`: LOW.** It appears once, while counting
candles on one example chart (*"one two three four five — the dealer shifts away one hour 15 minutes
after the high is locked"*). **Five 15-minute bars is 1h15m, so this is him reading that chart, not
stating a rule.** Any artifact treating it as a parameter would be over-reading a worked example.

### 2.6 Star formations

> A star formation is **not a shape signal**. It is a railroad-track pair with an extra 15-minute
> candle inserted, and the signal is entirely in **the next 15 minutes**.

**CONFIDENCE: CERTAIN** that this is what is taught. `[01:04:01]` *"This formation does not
matter"*; `[01:04:18]` *"What matters is … the next 15 minutes"*; `[01:04:26]` the deliberately
absurd list (*"orange hearts blue stars green clovers, I don't care"*); `[01:06:54]` *"a railroad
track broken into four pieces"*; and the definition printed at `62:44` — *"Simply put : An extra 15M
for the Market Maker to take your money."*

⭐ **This is the most interesting claim in the lesson because it inverts standard candlestick
teaching.** The named pattern is explicitly stripped of predictive content and replaced with a
timing test. **It remains untested.** `PT-047` / `BT_V19_0001.md` tests the second-leg time cap and
the `25–50` extension; `PT-047` §1a expressly excludes the M/W formation and no star formation is
measured anywhere in that runner (R1 item 304).

### 2.7 Position sizing

> Fix the risk percentage; let the stop distance set the lot size. Do not skip a valid structure
> because its stop is wide.

**CONFIDENCE: CERTAIN.** `[00:58:20]`–`[00:58:37]` states the equivalence and answers it (*"answer
yes"*); `[01:00:12]` gives the worked instruction (*"if you're a two percent trader and this trade
comes in at three percent, scale back the number of lots to make this a two percent risk trade, but
take it because it's a good structure"*).

⭐ **This is the only rule in V19 that is fully mechanical with no undefined term in it.** It needs
no moving average, no nickname, no colour and no indicator. **It is therefore the only V19 rule that
`D-030` does not block** — and it is a money-management rule, not an entry rule.

### 2.8 TDI

> The TDI's operative line is the **RSI line**; it is computed on **closes**; divergence between it
> and price marks a stop hunt that makes a higher high on a lower close.

**CONFIDENCE: HIGH.** `[00:27:07]`, `[00:27:18]`, `[00:27:25]`, `[00:56:25]`–`[00:56:30]`.

⭐ **Consistent with the owner's 2026-08-14 ruling that the volatility bands are a deviation of the
RSI line** — being recorded by a parallel session as `D-052`, and **not yet on the commit this branch
forked from**. Cited here as a recent owner ruling **pending its own commit**, and **not
re-litigated**.

⚠⚠ **BUT V19 CANNOT BE OFFERED AS EVIDENCE *FOR* THE BAND BASIS, AND IS NOT.** V19 says nothing
about the bands: *"band"*, *"volatility band"* and *"shark fin"* return **zero**. What V19
establishes is narrower and still useful — **that the RSI line is the close-based line the course
reads divergence off**. That is consistent with the ruling and independent of it.

### 2.9 The entry rule

> Enter when the dealer closes back below the **blue tracer** and forms the M **in the mustard**, or
> **collapses inside the mustard**.

**CONFIDENCE that this is the stated entry: HIGH** (`[00:31:29]`, `[00:39:56]`).
**CONFIDENCE that it can be implemented: BLOCKED.** ⚠⚠ *"Blue tracer"* is spoken 15 times and is
**never defined**; the charts carry two candidate blue-family lines and no frame prints a period
beside a colour. *"Mustard"* is bound by `D-043` to a **colour**, and V19 supplies no period.

**`D-030` applies without qualification: the definitions are not approximated.** `A-133`.
