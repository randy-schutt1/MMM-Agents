## Q3 — Is the ADR lesson a deployable method, or a heuristic?

### **A HEURISTIC, and the lesson says so itself. Confidence: HIGH.**

The printed rule is `V15_00-41-30_…png`:

> `Yesterday's High And Low, A Pivot Point And The ADR Marker Will Create Strong Intra-day
> Reversal Zones` / `Especially If These Points Also Coincide With A Longer Period Moving AVG
> 50 Or 200`

**Everything needed to *draw* the zone is specified.** Four objects, all computable: yesterday's
high and low; a pivot point; the ADR high/low; a 50 or 200 EMA. The spoken list at `[00:42:45]`
adds a fifth, the TDI shark fin, and a time, `9.30`.

**Nothing needed to *trade* it is specified.** No entry trigger beyond *"the dealer makes the M or
the W"*; no stop; no target beyond *"acts as a target once the trade is on"*; no tolerance for
what *"coincide"* means in pips; no rule for what to do when three of the four line up and one
does not; no timezone on `9.30`.

**And the lesson disclaims it in its own voice**, `[00:29:08]`: *"Is that always true? **No.** But
this should be part of your subset of rules for you to take those trades."*

⭐ **Two of the four legs are attributed to STUDENTS, not to the instructor's doctrine** —
`[00:29:25]` *"**Zen** has his checklist… part of his checklist… is that price has met or exceeded
the ADR"* and `[00:30:27]` *"**Asubio** puts it as they should get your attention."* The second is
explicitly a **softening**: the instructor adopts *"gets your attention"* over anything stronger.
**No spec file in this project should render the ADR filter as a gate.** It is a watchlist rule.

**What is genuinely new to the corpus here**, and it is not nothing:
