## 5. SEQUENCE / PROCEDURE TAUGHT

The one procedure that is stated end-to-end in this video is **reading the week**, not
taking a trade. Assembled from `[00:08:57]`–`[00:22:21]` and `[00:28:26]`–`[00:30:06]`:

```text
1. Identify Sunday/Monday as the accumulation phase of the week
   ("the Asian session for the week").                          [00:09:29]
2. Wait for the dealer's false move out of that range — the
   aggressive spike that traps traders. This is the weekly
   equivalent of the intraday stop hunt.                        [00:09:51]
3. Locate the anchor point: the midweek reversal, where the
   dealer "locks in the level".                                 [00:04:15]
4. From the anchor point, the trend for the next 2.5–4 days is
   known.                                                       [00:16:23]
5. Expect the dealer NOT to cross that level for 2.5–4 days.    [00:17:09]
6. Project the extent with ADR × 3 (three cycles, not three
   days).                                                       [00:05:58]
7. Watch for outside structure high/low as the first sign the
   run is over.                                                 [00:20:21]
8. On outside structure high, expect one of exactly two things:
   half-Batman to the low, OR finish the M formation in London
   and correct.                                                 [00:20:33]
9. Get out on Friday. "You always get out on Friday, my friend,
   always."                                                     [00:05:24]
```

**This is a map-reading procedure, not an execution procedure.** It contains no entry
price, no stop, and no position size. Where an execution step would go, the lesson
substitutes "wait for a second leg" — which it does not define verbally.
