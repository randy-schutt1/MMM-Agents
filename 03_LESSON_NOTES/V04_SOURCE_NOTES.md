## 1. LESSON OVERVIEW

Week 2, part 2 of 3 (V03–V05 share the 2012-03-25 session date; V03 is part 1). Two
segments with a hard seam:

**Segment A — the instructor, `[00:00:00]`–`[00:26:56]`.** A short, dense, unusually
prescriptive block. Its spine is a single prohibition and the criteria that lift it:
*do not take M/W setups inside the Asian range; wait until the dealer hits the stops

chart he draws on for ~22 minutes. Closes by assigning the week's homework.

does not teach in this segment.

## 5. SEQUENCE / PROCEDURE TAUGHT (instructor)

1. Mark the four-hour chart; block off the **first eight hours of the week**; draw those
   levels across the chart `[00:16:06]`–`[00:16:15]`.
2. Identify the **high of the week and low of the week** `[00:18:24]`.
3. Drill down to the **15-minute** chart and find the sub-pattern — the M/W that coincides
   with the weekly extreme `[00:18:33]`–`[00:18:58]`.
4. Wait. **Do not trade inside the blue box** `[00:07:19]`.
5. Wait for the dealer to **hit the stops** — a move **25–50 pips beyond the box**
   `[00:15:43]`.
6. Look for the **second leg** of the M/W failing to break the extreme `[00:09:13]`.
7. Confirm on **TDI** — shark fin outside the band, back in; blood in the water
   `[00:15:49]`–`[00:15:55]`.
8. Enter at/near the **second leg's close** `[00:08:56]`.
9. Stop **10–18 pips** (7–10 on the second leg) `[00:04:43]`, `[00:04:11]`.
10. If stopped and the pattern re-forms at the next level, **re-enter** `[00:03:48]`.
11. Then **trade away from the weekly high for 2.5–3 days** until the next signal
    `[00:20:28]`.
