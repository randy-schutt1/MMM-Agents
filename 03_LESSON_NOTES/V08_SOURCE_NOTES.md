## 5. THE HARD-RIGHT-EDGE FLASHCARD METHOD — `GUEST`

> `[00:27:50]`–`[00:27:55]` *"All of a sudden you wake up and realize **I can see him in W

### 5b. The technique

| Step | His words | Marker |
|---|---|---|
| Take a completed setup and truncate it | *"we take an ideal setup and then **we chop it back**. So we're now looking at the setup as it would be at the best possible entry on the hard right edge"* | `[00:28:00]`–`[00:28:11]` |

| Play them forward one at a time | *"These are **many flashcards strung together**. I'm just clicking this **forward button** to move through it"* | `[00:33:57]`–`[00:33:59]` |
