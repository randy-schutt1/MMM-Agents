## 1. LESSON OVERVIEW

Week 2, part 1 of 3 (V03–V05 share the 2012-03-25 session date). ~12 minutes of
housekeeping and mail, then three teaching blocks:

1. **The weekly cycle on the four-hour chart** (`[00:12:38]`–`[00:40:31]`) — the
   intraday pattern taught in week 1, re-taught at week scale: block the first eight
   hours, treat them as the week's accumulation phase, and read the rest of the week
   as trading away from and back to those levels.
2. **Flashcards** (`[00:40:57]`–`[00:58:14]`) — how to turn winning setups into
   annotated pattern cards (MS Paint walkthrough), what to note on each card, and a
   long run of the instructor's own cards read aloud.
3. **Q&A** (`[00:58:18]`–end) — quarter-of-wood, fractional disparity, TDI position,
   flashcard timeframes, the work-time-ribbon support saga, risk limits.

The printed agenda (`V03_00-12-09_agenda-slide…png`) also lists **"Trading Zone"**;
the recording ends without teaching it. See §14 and A-005.

The stated purpose of the four-hour framing: *"Start with a big picture and scale it
down, crank down your microscope"* `[00:22:07]`; *"The biggest problem is
understanding where the peaks are, the high, the weak [week], low, the weak, and
trading away from those areas"* `[00:22:21]`.

## 5. SEQUENCE / PROCEDURE TAUGHT

The weekly-cycle reading procedure, as sequenced by the lesson:

1. Open the four-hour chart (one-hour permitted if you can see it — `[00:28:18]`).
2. Block the first eight hours of the week; mark their high and low
   (`[00:12:46]`–`[00:12:58]`; horizontal lines `[00:23:10]`–`[00:23:22]`).
3. Treat those as the week's psychological support/resistance and the market-maker
   spread (`[00:13:22]`, `[00:29:13]`).
4. Expect the dealer to cut one side (false move) — that gives the directional bias
   (`[00:23:33]`–`[00:24:03]`; railroad tracks on the 4h also give bias `[00:27:51]`).
5. Expect the return to the level and the stop-run — the M/W second leg is the entry
   moment (`[00:24:22]`–`[00:24:47]`).
6. Trade away from the week's peak for ~2.5–3 days / up to three levels; watch for
   3×ADR (`[00:34:58]`, `[00:35:36]`–`[00:36:16]`).
7. At the 3-day window plus an outside-structure extreme: take profit; the instructor
   says "go the other way" (`[00:36:16]`–`[00:36:24]`).

Flashcard procedure: §2h. Card-matching procedure: §2i.
