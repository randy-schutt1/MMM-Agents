## 1. WORKING UNDERSTANDING — WHAT THIS LESSON IS ABOUT

V08 is a **training-methodology lesson wearing a motivational-talk costume**, and reading it as
one or the other loses half of it.

The costume is real: roughly the first 25 minutes are an argument about **why students fail to
trust the method**, with a four-stage psychological model, a claim about the 19th-century
education system, and a gallery of last week's charts offered as a rebuttal to doubt. Nothing in
that stretch is a trading rule.

But the argument has a **payload**, and the payload is the lesson's contribution:

> `[00:27:50]` *"I can see him in W patterns clearly after they form, but I can't see him before
> they form."*

That sentence names the gap between **recognising a pattern** and **trading one** — a gap the
corpus has circled since V04 without naming. V08's two techniques are both attacks on it:

| Technique | What it attacks | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard-right-edge flashcards** — truncate a known setup, replay it forward one candle at a time | *I can't see it before it forms* | §5 |
| **The high-low drill** — practise entering at the day's extreme, on demo, without confirmation | *my entry is too late and my stop is too wide* | §6 |

**The second is the important one for this project**, because it is the first time anyone in the
corpus has explained what "Hi-Lo" actually asks a trader to do.

## 2. WHAT V08 CONTRIBUTES TO THE CORPUS

### 2.1 The training method is separable from the trading method `EXPLICIT`

V08 draws a line the corpus did not have: `[00:39:23]` *"this is your **isolated skill** that you
want to learn in addition to the other aspects that we're showing you with this teaching"*, and
`[00:39:00]` *"**Take your trades at confirmed entries. Yes** — but make sure that when you're
trying to catch these lows, **you do this on demo**."*

So the high-low drill is **two things at once** and the lesson keeps them apart:

1. A **practice regime**, run on demo, whose output is a trained eye.
2. An **entry style** that the trained eye eventually makes available live.

`[00:39:32]` gives the transition and it is gradual, not a switch: *"When pulling it into live
trading, the effects of this training are that **entries will start to migrate toward the
extremes of M and W formations**."*

**This matters for how the corpus tests V08 at all.** A practice regime is not falsifiable against
price data; an entry style is. Conflating them would produce a test of something the lesson never
claimed. See §6.

### 2.2 `A-056` is EXTENDED and I am not closing it `EXPLICIT` + `UNRESOLVED`

`A-056` records that V07 named "Hi-Lo" as a primary method, recommended it in print, and never
taught it. V08 supplies three of the four things a rule needs:

| Component | V08's answer | Marker |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** to enter | at the extreme of the day's high or low | `[00:36:27]`, `[00:37:41]` |
| **Where in the structure** | in the second leg, at an area previously defined as a trap area | `[00:37:51]`, `[00:41:38]` |
| **What cues it** | speed — *"when they move this fast down here really fast, that's your cue"* | `[00:36:50]` |
| **How to know it is the extreme before it is** | **still missing** | — |

**The fourth is the whole problem and V08 does not solve it.** *"Fast"* has no definition
anywhere in the lesson (`A-061`), and speed is the only forward-looking element offered. The
honest statement is that V08 turns **Hi-Lo exists and someone is good at it** into **Hi-Lo means
entering at the extreme in the second leg, cued by speed** — which is a real advance and is still
not executable. (Both phrasings are mine, not the lesson's; they are set in bold rather than in
quotation marks for that reason.)

**I therefore extend `A-056` and decline to close it.** Under `D-033` a guest statement *can* now
close a record, so the reason for not closing is evidentiary and not procedural: **the record
asks how the extreme is identified in advance, and the lesson answers with an undefined
adjective.** Closing it on `[00:36:50]` would substitute my reading of *fast* for the course's,
which is `D-030` exactly.

### 2.3 The confirmation requirement is called a myth, by a normative speaker `EXPLICIT`

The corpus's confirmation rule has been stable since V04. V08 states it, attributes it, and then
overrides it:

> `[00:37:07]` *"**In our basic training, we do say that you want to have a confirmation
> candle.** There's a reversal candle and a candle in the direction that you want to go. We say
> start there. Enter after you see these two."*
>
> `[00:36:58]` *"The high low drill also flushes out the big trading **myth** that one needs
> confirmation in the same direction to enter a trade."*

Two things follow, and they pull in opposite directions.

**First, this is the clearest statement of the confirmation rule in the corpus.** *A reversal
candle, then a candle in the direction you want to go* is a two-element sequence, stated as
doctrine, attributed to *"our basic training"*. It does not define either candle's shape, so it
does not close anything — but as a statement of the rule's **structure** it is better than what
V04–V07 give.

**Second, it is a genuine conflict.** Under `D-025` this would have been a corpus-hygiene note.
Under **`D-033` provision 3** — which inverts that — a guest and the course stating incompatible
rules is a **method-level contradiction**. Logged as `C-009`.

**How much of a conflict?** Less than it first looks, and I want to be accurate rather than
dramatic about it. The lesson supplies its own reconciliation at `[00:39:00]`: confirmed entries
live, extreme entries on demo, until the skill is trained. That is a **staging rule**, and it
resolves what a student should *do* this week. What it does not resolve is which entry is
**correct** for a trained trader — V08 says the extreme is *"actually the safest place to enter
trades"* `[00:37:27]`, which is not a hedge. I have recorded `C-009` as `PROVISIONAL` on that
basis: real conflict, with an explicit staging rule that defers rather than dissolves it.

## 5. TENSIONS AND WHAT I DID NOT RESOLVE

### 5.1 The lesson's epistemology argues against the lesson's evidence `UNRESOLVED`

This is the sharpest thing I noticed and I do not think it is a trivial gotcha.

V08's entire first half argues that **you must not accept a claim on authority — you must check
it with your own eyes**: `[00:13:05]` *"we have been conditioned since early childhood to believe
authority is the truth and to not seek truth as the authority"*; `[00:14:26]` *"no one else can
validate the method for you… **Do it yourself.**"*

Then the validation it offers is **a gallery of 29 charts, pre-selected and narrated by the
speaker**, with the outcome already visible on every one. `[00:23:59]` *"Are you still doubting?
I hope not. You'd have to be kind of silly to doubt this stuff at this point."*

**That is an appeal to authority in the middle of an argument against appeals to authority** — and
the lesson does not notice. To be fair to it: the gallery is explicitly framed as a *demonstration
that the exercise is worth doing*, and the exercise he assigns (`R1`) is genuinely the
do-it-yourself version. So the charge is not hypocrisy. It is that **the persuasive weight of the
lesson sits on the gallery, and the gallery is exactly the kind of evidence the lesson tells you
not to trust.**

I record this as an observation about the lesson's structure, not as a finding against the method.
It is also, for this project, a **methodological warning**: a curated gallery of winners is
`E09` (cherry-picking) by construction, and the corpus must not treat the 29 as a measurement.

### 5.2 *"Go off my faith here"*, four sentences before *"big scientific reason"* `EXPLICIT`

> `[00:29:48]` *"I'm not going to go into why and how and all this stuff and **the studies that
> actually validate this** and **go off my faith here**."*
>
> `[00:31:16]` *"**Big scientific reason** that we're doing it this way guys."*

He claims studies exist, declines to cite them, says he is going on faith, and then calls the
mechanism scientific. Logged as `C-008`.

**What I am *not* saying:** that the flashcard drill does not work. Deliberate practice with rapid

the chart at the decision point, replay forward, judge before the outcome — is, structurally, the
same discipline `D-027` imposes on backtesting. **The drill may be excellent and the justification
offered for it still unevidenced.** Those are separate questions and the lesson merges them.

## 6. WHY `PT-034` TESTS WHAT IT TESTS — THE `D-030` ARGUMENT

Recorded here, before the pre-registration, so the reasoning can be audited separately from the
result.

**Almost everything in V08 is blocked.** `R1`, `R4`, `R5`, `R8` need M/W anatomy, second leg,
trap area or a definition of *fast* — `A-011`, `A-007`, `A-002`, `A-061`. `R2` and `R6` are study
procedures and make no market claim. `R9` is a size without a placement rule. `R10` is V04's
territory and already has `PT-018`.

**`R7` survives, and the reason is precise:**

> The **day's high and low are arithmetic** once a day boundary is fixed — `PT-033` established
> exactly this for V07 — and V08 supplies **both** of the numbers the R:R claim needs: the
> **50-pip target**, stated eight times as *"Easy 50"*, and the **3:1 ratio**, stated once and
> explicitly. Nothing about `R7` requires a course definition that does not exist.

**And `PT-034` is not a repeat of `PT-033`.** `PT-033` recorded, in its own §3, *"**No stop.** The
claim contains none, V07 states none, and inventing one would be `D-010`."* **V08 supplies the
stop** — implicitly, as target ÷ ratio — which is the missing half `PT-033` said it could not have.
That is the entire reason a new test is worth running:

| | `PT-033` (V07) | `PT-034` (V08) |
|---|---|---|
| Target | 50 pips, from the lesson | 50 pips, from **this** lesson, stated 8× |
| Stop | **none available** | **≤ 16.7 pips**, `IMPLIED` from *"three to one or greater"* |
| Question | can 50 pips be reached from the day's extreme at all? | **is the R:R actually 3:1 or better, and how fast does that decay with imprecision?** |

**What `PT-034` cannot do, stated before it runs:** it cannot show the high-low drill works, for
the same reason `PT-033` could not — the lesson supplies no way to identify the extreme in
advance, and `PT-034` does not invent one. At perfect hindsight the stop is unreachable *by
construction* within the day, so the interesting arm is the **imprecision** arm, and the headline
observable is the **measured** maximum adverse excursion rather than an imposed stop. Measuring
the achieved R:R rather than assuming one is what keeps `D-010` intact.

### 8.1 One reading is strengthened enough to change how a test is designed

`§2.2` concluded that V08 supplies three of the four components a Hi-Lo rule needs and not the
fourth. **That stands.** But frame `00-05-40` supplies something §2.2 did not have — a **number**:

> *"The High Low Drill and Elements of Mastery for dealing **within 10 pips of HOD/LOD**."*

The audio never says it, and the transcript garbles it (`intent pips` = *in ten pips*). So the
drill's **target tolerance** is specified after all, by a normative speaker, in print.

**This does not close `A-056` and I am not closing it.** §2.2's argument was that the missing
component is *how to know the extreme before it is the extreme*, and a tolerance on the target
does not supply that — knowing you want to be within 10 pips of the low tells you nothing about
which candle is the low. **The record asks for a method and this is a specification.**

**What it does change is `PT-034`.** The imprecision grid needs a tolerance parameter, and
`PT-033` had to invent its grid `X ∈ {0, 2, 5, 10}` because V07 offered only *"within a few
pips"*. **V08 supplies `10` explicitly, so `PT-034` takes its headline tolerance from the lesson
rather than from a session's judgement** — which removes a live `D-010` exposure from the design
rather than merely disclosing one. That is the single most useful thing the visual pass produced.

### 8.4 What I got wrong, stated plainly

Reading the terminal panel at half resolution, I first read `S/L 1.32360` against an entry of
`1.32424` as **a 6.4-pip initial stop** — which, next to a lesson claiming *"tighter stops…
3:1 or greater"*, looked like the corroborating number of the whole session.

**It is not that.** The closes are below the opens with positive profit, so both positions are
**shorts**, and for a short a stop **below** the entry is *in profit* — a trailed stop, not the

recovered from it.**

Recorded because the failure mode is the instructive part: the misreading was available at half
resolution and died at 3× magnification, which is the V06 review's lesson (*read frames at full
resolution before declaring anything about them*) arriving from the opposite direction — not a
detail wrongly dismissed as illegible, but **a legible detail wrongly interpreted**. The
prohibition written into `04_SCREENSHOTS/V08/INDEX.md` § "FRAME 14 IN DETAIL" exists so the next
session does not make the same inference from the same pixels.
