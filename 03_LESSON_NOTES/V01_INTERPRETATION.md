## 1. WORKING UNDERSTANDING

**Confidence for this summary as a whole: LOW-to-MEDIUM.**

V01 appears to be a framing lesson rather than a mechanics lesson. It argues a thesis
and gives prohibitions; it does not deliver an executable method.

The thesis, as I read it: price movement in a trading week is produced deliberately
by a "dealer" whose objective is to make retail traders hold the wrong position, and
that production follows a repeating structure. Traps occur at predictable *times* —
week open, each session open, each session close, week close. Somewhere in the middle
of the week the dealer establishes an "anchor point", after which direction is
committed for the remainder of the week. The trader's job is to identify where in
that structure the market currently sits, refuse the early trap, and take entries
only after a session has opened and only on a second leg.

What the lesson conspicuously does **not** provide: any definition of the anchor
point, any anatomy of the M or W formation, any description of what the "trading
zone" or "blue box" is, any indicator, any entry trigger, any stop placement, any
target, and any position size. §6 of the source notes — Conditions Stated — has an
empty Confirmation column and an empty Invalidation column for all eight rows. That
is not a gap in my note-taking; the material is not present.

Two structural reasons for that emptiness, and they matter differently:

1. **Deferral.** The trading zone is explicitly promised for later the same evening
   (`[00:43:35]`, `[00:44:22]`), i.e. in V02. This is the instructor sequencing his
   own material, and it is fine.
2. **Assumed prior knowledge.** At `[00:34:33]` he says "**remember** we have the
   trap moves…", and at `[00:36:07]` he quotes students quoting his own earlier
   statement of the day count. V01 is the start of *this bootcamp cycle*, not the
   start of his teaching. Some vocabulary used here — peak formation, M and W, level,
   tracer — may be assumed from material outside this 21-video library entirely.

The most quotable single line, `[00:52:38]` "same shit every week, the only difference
between one week to the next is where the tracer falls", is also the least
operational: it asserts the pattern is invariant while leaving every component of the
pattern undefined.
