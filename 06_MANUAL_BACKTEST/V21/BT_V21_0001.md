## §4 — WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**Establishes:** on GBP/USD M15, 2014–2015 development data, both `D-031` arms, the median forward
excursion above an exceeded 8–24-bar-old day-high is **23–30 pips**, about **double** a matched
random window's, and **the number of a 20/20-spaced grid's orders touched is not stable across
windows.**

**Does NOT establish:**

* ⛔ **that V21's *"about 50 pips"* is wrong about what the instructor means.** ⚠️ **The event is
  `PT-047`'s convention, not V21's** (`PT-050` §2a) — V21 says *"stop hunt"* and never defines one.
  **A different stop-hunt definition would produce a different number, and this test cannot exclude
  one that yields 50.**
* ⛔ **anything about the script.** The artifact is not in this repository (`A-141`).
* ⛔ **anything about *"lose at a discount"***, which is arithmetic (`A-140`).
* ⚠️ **that any order would have FILLED.** *"Touched"* ignores spread and slippage (`PT-050` §6.3).
* ⚠️ **anything out of sample** — `W-B` contains `W-A`; the `D-035` holdout was untouched.

## §5 — RUNNER / PRE-REGISTRATION DISAGREEMENTS

> ### ⚠️⚠️ CORRECTED 2026-08-15 — V21 R1 item **367**. **THIS SECTION ORIGINALLY SAID *"NONE"*.**

**⛔ ONE DISAGREEMENT, AND IT IS THE ONE THAT PRODUCED THE VERDICT WORD.**

`PT-050` §4's fourth `N3` condition reads: *"the modal fill cell differs **between arms**"*.
⛔ **The runner compares every cell to the primary cell**, so it fires on **window** differences as
well as arm differences — and **both fires in `pt050_results.json` are window differences**
(`A|W-B`, `B|W-B` against `A|W-A`).

**On the literal list, `N3` does not fire:**

| §4 condition | Literal reading |
|---|---|
| arms A/B disagree on band membership | ✅ no — all four cells agree the median is **out** of band |
| `W-A` / `W-B` disagree | ⚠️ **§4 clause 2 does not say what they must disagree ON**; on band membership they agree |
| any cell `n < 30` | ✅ no — 99 / 206 / 98 / 207 |
| **modal fill differs BETWEEN ARMS** | ✅ **no — arms agree in every window: `W-A` 3/3, `W-B` 1/1** |

⭐⭐ **SO ON THE LITERAL LIST `PT-050` §5 GIVES `REFUTED`, NOT `FRAGILE`** — the median's interval
`[23.20, 39.60]` lies outside `[40, 60]`, which is §5's `REFUTED` row.

⚠️ **THE PRE-REGISTRATION GOVERNS AND IS NOT EDITED** (`COMMON_PROTOCOL.md` §9 rule 7), **and the
runner is NOT re-run** — V21 R1 item 367 says so explicitly. **What was owed was the disagreement
report, and this is it.**

⭐ **Two things in mitigation, and the reviewer states both first.** **(1)** §4 clause 2 (*"`W-A` and
`W-B` disagree"*) is **genuinely ambiguous about what must disagree**, so a window-sensitive reading
of the guard is defensible — it is the unexercised **governance clause** that is charged, not the
implementation. **(2)** ⭐ **The direction is conservative:** `FRAGILE` is reported as a null and
`REFUTED` is a stronger negative, so **the error understated the result against the lesson rather
than for it.** ⚠️ **The interval misses the band by 0.4 pips, so the distance between the two
verdicts is small in substance and large in wording.**

⛔ **EITHER WAY THE CLAIM IS NOT SUPPORTED**, and §0's headline figures are unaffected.
