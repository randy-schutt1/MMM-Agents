## §4 — WHAT THE RESULT ACTUALLY SAYS

### ⭐ 4a. The distance IS there, and it is emphatically not what random entry gets

The **median** favourable excursion from the extreme is **56.8 / 52.9 pips** — i.e. **the median
case clears the speaker's 50**, and `O3`'s bootstrap CI excludes anything below ~41 pips. Against a
same-metric random-origin control on the same days and directions, `P(MFE ≥ 50)` is **0.094 /
0.101**. **The rule population reaches 50 pips roughly five to six times as often as a random entry
on the same day in the same direction, a gap of +42 to +45 pp against a threshold of +10.**

**That is a large, arm-consistent effect and it is the strongest quantitative result this project
has obtained on a V13 claim.** It says the *"50 to 100 pips on the table"* framing is describing
something real about the post-stop-hunt path.

### ⚠ 4b. But the two specific numbers he gives are stated far more confidently than they hold

*"**You'll hit your 50 pips. You'll hit 40**"* is the language of an expectation. Measured, **under
perfect entry**, it happens **54% / 52%** of the time for 50 pips and **63% / 64%** for 40.

**A coin-flip dressed as a floor.** And this is the **upper bound** — a real entry is worse.

### ⭐⭐ 4c. The most important row is `O4`, and it is the one a careless reader would skip

*"and the dealer comes back into the Asian levels"* is **the claim's antecedent**, not decoration —
it is the mechanism by which the pips are supposed to arrive. **It holds 70% of the time, in both
arms, against a pre-registered 80%.**

> **`O4` was computed FIRST by pre-registered run order (`PT-041` §7 step 5), precisely so it could
> not become an afterthought once the headline rates were known.** Nearly **a third** of qualifying
> days never see price return into the box at all. On those days the arithmetic does not fail
> *narrowly* — its premise is simply absent.

### 4d. An incidental `D-031` observation, not a result of this test

**The DST arm changes the median box width materially: 31.0 pips (arm A) vs 26.7 pips (arm B)**,
which moves the 25–50 filter's yield from 56.8% to 49.2% of days. **This is not a finding about
V13's claim** — it is a reminder that `D-031`'s arms are not cosmetic, and that any test filtering
on box width inherits an arm dependency before it measures anything. **The outcome measures
themselves are strikingly arm-stable** (`O1` 0.543/0.522, `O2` 0.630/0.642, `O4` 0.704/0.701),
which is the more reassuring half of the same observation.

## §5 — ⚠ A DEFECT IN THE PRE-REGISTRATION, EXPOSED BY THE RUN AND DISCLOSED

**`PT-041` §5 named the wrong control, and the error would have flattered the claim.**

§5 specified `mmm_lib.n1_matched_random` over the `TradeGrid` — but the grid's `mfe_pips` is
**truncated by the stop/target resolution**, so it is bounded above, while `O1`/`O2` measure an
**unbounded** same-day excursion. Comparing them is not like-for-like, and the direction of the
error is **against the control**: it would have made the baseline look smaller than it is.

**Both controls were therefore run and both are reported:**

| Control | Arm A | Arm B | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`C-PRE`** — as pre-registered, `TradeGrid.mfe_pips` | median **17.59** [14.37, 20.67] | median **18.25** [14.9, 21.89] | ⚠ **Not like-for-like. Reported for completeness, NOT used for the verdict** |
| **`C-LIKE`** — added at run time: random post-box origin bar, **same day, same direction, identical MFE code path** | `P(≥50)` **0.094**, `P(≥40)` **0.167**, median **14.4** | `P(≥50)` **0.101**, `P(≥40)` **0.176**, median **15.3** | ✅ **The clause is adjudicated on this** |

> **`C-LIKE` is STRICTER than what was pre-registered, and adding it makes the claim HARDER to
> support, not easier.** That is why it is a disclosed amendment rather than a post-hoc loosening.
> **A reviewer should nonetheless treat the §6 control clause as having been adjudicated on a
> control the pre-registration did not name**, and is entitled to discount it on that basis. The
> verdict does not turn on it: `PARTIALLY SUPPORTED` is driven by `O4` and `O2` failing their
> thresholds, and **both of those are pure rule-arm measurements with no control involved.**

**Recorded as an open item for R1.** `PT-041` §5 should be amended for any re-issue.
