# BT_V03_0003 — PT-026: is the first-eight-hours range of the week cut?

> ## ⚠ MANDATORY SCOPE STATEMENT — `PT-026` §6, verbatim
>
> **PT-026 tests whether the first-eight-hours range of the week is breached, when and how
> far.** It is **not** a test of the anchor point (`A-001`), the level (`A-004`), the M/W
> (`A-011`) or any entry. It measures a drawn range and reports what price did to it.
>
> It **re-issues `PT-008`** onto `W-C′` under `D-035`; `PT-008` is retained, marked and never
> run, and no result here may be reported as `PT-008`'s result.
>
> **Price levels on this corpus are not comparable with the V02–V06 FXCM homework** (`D-036a`);
> only shape and distance claims travel. The **week open is 22:00 UTC**, not FXCM's 21:00 UTC.
> The instructor's *"eight hours"* and *"two bars"* are **different spans on this corpus** and
> both are reported; neither is adopted as the definition.

## 4. THE BASELINES — AND A STRUCTURAL CONFOUND IN THE PRE-REGISTERED SWEEP

### 4a. The confound, and the matched basis added alongside

The pre-registered second baseline ranks the week-open block among **8-hour blocks at every
4-hour offset through the week**. Measured as written:

```text
week-open block 'both edges cut' = 0.817  ->  RANK 1 OF 30
```

**That rank is an artifact and it must not be reported as a result.** The outcome window
shrinks with the offset:

| Offset | Outcome window | measurable weeks | 'both cut' |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 h** | **~112 h** | 180 | **0.817** |
| +8 h | ~104 h | 180 | 0.639 |
| +104 h | ~8 h | 178 | 0.022 |
| +108 h | ~4 h | 178 | 0.000 |
| +112 h, +116 h | — | **0 — dropped, counted** | n/a |

**A block with 112 hours to be breached in will be breached more often than one with 4 hours,
whatever the market does.** So the week-open block would rank first however GBP/USD behaved.
Note also that offsets **+20 h and +24 h tie it at 0.817** even on the confounded basis.

**A matched-basis sweep was added ALONGSIDE the pre-registered one** — the same 24-hour
outcome window measured after every offset's own block close. **The pre-registered sweep is
retained and reported, not replaced**; `D-027` forbids editing a pre-registration into
conformance, and this is the same handling the earlier batch gave PT-003's structural
confound (`9eb2d0c`).

```text
MATCHED BASIS — same 24 h outcome window for every offset
  week-open block 'both edges cut' = 0.528 (n = 180)
  ->  RANK 7 OF 23 measurable offsets,  against a sweep median of 0.315
```

> ### **Rank 1 of 30 becomes rank 7 of 23 once the outcome window is matched. The week's opening eight hours are cut somewhat more often than a typical arbitrary eight-hour block, and are nowhere near the most-cut block in the week.**
>
> This is the single most consequential number in the observation, and the pre-registered
> sweep could not have produced it.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 'both edges cut' | 0.817 | 0.680 | [0.494, 0.860] | **88.2** |
| 'neither edge cut' | 0.000 | 0.000 | [0, 0.006] | **0.0** |
| median hours to first breach | 1.25 | 2.25 | [0.25, 52.89] | **28.4** |

**Identical on both `D-031` arms**, for the structural reason in §2.

> **The week-open block does not clear N3 at the 95th percentile on any measure** (88.2 is
> the closest). `PT-026` §5 row 1 is the applicable reading: *"The week-open block is breached
> at a rate indistinguishable from shifted blocks → **The week's opening range carries no
> special status at this sample.** A foundational null for the weekly half of the method —
> report prominently."*
>
> **The breach TIMING is the one place the block looks unusual in the right direction** —
> median 1.25 h against N3's 2.25 h, percentile 28.4 (i.e. faster than ~72% of shifted
> anchors). That is §5 row 2's outcome — *"Breach rate is ordinary but breach timing is not …
> A narrower, real finding, and the more likely one"* — and it does **not** clear a
> pre-registered threshold either. **Reported as an observation, not as a finding.**

### 4c. The width sweep — the artifact it was built to catch is real

| Block width | 'both cut' | 'neither cut' | median hours to breach |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 h | 0.861 | 0.000 | 1.25 |
| **8 h** | **0.817** | **0.000** | **1.25** |
| 12 h | 0.678 | 0.000 | 1.25 |
| 24 h | 0.483 | 0.000 | 11.12 |

**Breach rate falls monotonically with block width, exactly as the third baseline was
pre-registered to expose.** The 8-hour block's 0.817 is **not** evidence about eight hours;
it is what an 8-hour-wide range gives. **`NEITHER` is 0.000 at every width** — even a
24-hour opening range is cut in every week of the sample.

## 8. RULE APPLICATION VERSUS OUTCOME

The decision point is the **block close**, and **nothing after it informed the block**: the
block high and low are computed from bars strictly inside the block window, and every measure
after it is an outcome. **All three baselines were computed and printed before the week-open
block's numbers were read** (§7 step 5); the run order in `data/pt026_report.txt` is the print
order. Week boundaries came from the Sunday-17:00 anchor and **never from `C7`** (step 3),
asserted by the runner. §3c's dispositions were applied **by name**. **All three block arms are
reported whatever they showed** — §0c requires it and *"reporting only the block that worked is
`E09`."* Neither `PT-026` nor `PT-008` was edited.

**One addition, disclosed rather than folded in:** the matched-basis sweep of §4a is **not**
pre-registered. It was added because the pre-registered sweep is structurally confounded, it
is reported **alongside** the pre-registered version rather than instead of it, and **both
ranks appear in this file.**

## 9. WHAT THIS OBSERVATION DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**Does:**
- **The week's opening range is cut in 100% of measurable weeks** (0/180 uncut, on all three
  block arms, both `D-031` arms, and at every width from 4 h to 24 h). *"Must cut"* is
  **true and uninformative** — it excludes nothing.
- **It is not cut because it is the week's opening range.** Against N3 the block does not clear
  the 95th percentile on any measure (best 88.2), and on a matched outcome window it ranks
  **7 of 23** arbitrary offsets against a sweep median of 0.315.
- **Documents that the pre-registered 30-offset sweep is structurally confounded** by outcome-
  window length, and that its "rank 1 of 30" is an artifact — with the matched-basis rank
  reported beside it.
- Confirms **§0c's advance prediction** exactly: the two phrasings are 8 h and 7 h on Arm A,
  8 h and 6 h on Arm B.
- **Supplies `PT-027` its block**, and its handoff number: the week's extreme lands on the
  first-breach side in **0.589 (106/180)** of weeks — reported here as an **association**, not
  as a rule.

**Does not:**
- Test the anchor point (`A-001`), the level (`A-004`), the M/W (`A-011`), or any entry.
- Adopt either of the instructor's two phrasings as *the* definition (`D-030`).
- Support the faster breach timing (1.25 h vs N3's 2.25 h) as a finding — it clears no
  pre-registered threshold.
- Establish anything about **whether the forbidden trade loses**. That is `PT-027`.
- Generalise beyond GBP/USD and 2013-01-06 → 2016-06-30. **The 2017 regime and the October
  2016 flash crash are in HOLDOUT and were not opened.**
- Permit comparison against an FXCM-sourced block: **`I-010` Q1 is OPEN** and §3a forbids it
  until it closes.
