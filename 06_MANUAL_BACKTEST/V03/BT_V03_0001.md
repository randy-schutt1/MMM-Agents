## 5. THE SECOND BASELINE DECIDES IT — 50 SEPARATES NOTHING THAT THE MEDIAN DOES NOT

The random-median split has **no doctrinal claim attached**. If it separates outcomes as well
as the instructor's number, then 50 is doing nothing that "big box vs small box" does not
already do (`PT-016` §4).

| Separation (arm L − arm H) | **Arm A**: 50-pip cut | **Arm A**: median cut (31.0) | **Arm B**: 50-pip cut | **Arm B**: median cut (26.7) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| median excursion | −8.40 | **−12.35** | −6.50 | **−10.10** |
| share breaching both edges | +0.257 | +0.214 | **+0.432** | +0.197 |
| hit rate | −0.052 | −0.034 | −0.083 | **+0.027** |
| expectancy (pips) | −1.20 | **+1.90** | −2.59 | **+3.96** |

> ### **The meaningless split separates the outcome measures *better* and *in the claimed
> direction*; the instructor's split separates them worse and backwards.**
>
> On expectancy — the measure a filter is supposed to improve — the median cut favours small
> boxes by +1.90 / +3.96 pips (the direction *"less than 50"* predicts), while the 50-pip cut
> favours **large** boxes by 1.20 / 2.59 pips. On hit rate the 50-pip cut is negative on both
> arms; the median cut is ~0.
>
> The only measure on which 50 outperforms the median cut is *"both extremes beyond the box"*
> (Arm B, +0.432 vs +0.197) — and that is a near-mechanical consequence of box size, not an
> outcome: a wider box is harder to clear on both sides.
>
> **`50` is not a special number in this data.** Whatever separation exists is "big box vs
> small box", it is weak, and the doctrinal cut is a worse place to make it than the median.

## 6. THE THIRD BASELINE — VOLATILITY CONTROL

| | Arm A | Arm B |
|---|---|---|
| Raw median excursion, L vs H | 50.6 vs 58.9 (diff −8.4) | 52.6 vs 59.1 (diff −6.5) |
| Permutation two-sided p (raw) | **0.102** | **0.359** |
| Normalised (exc / day's own TR), L vs H | 0.580 vs 0.439 (diff +0.141) | 0.595 vs 0.507 (diff +0.088) |
| Permutation two-sided p (normalised) | **0.000** | **0.013** |

**The raw difference is not significant on either arm. The normalised difference is — and it
is close to an identity, not a finding.** `excursion / day-range` must fall as the box grows,
because the box is inside the day's range: a bigger box consumes more of the day and leaves
less outside it. `PT-016` §4 asked for this arm because *"box range is correlated with the
day's eventual range almost mechanically"*, and the arm confirms the mechanism rather than
revealing an effect.

**The honest summary of measure (a): days with small boxes do not travel further beyond them
in absolute pips (p = 0.102 / 0.359); they travel a larger fraction of a day that is itself
smaller.**

## 7. `D-031` — THE ARMS AGREE ON THE VERDICT AND DISAGREE ON THE INPUT

The verdict is identical on both arms: no separation, point estimates against the claim,
nothing clearing N1, no inflection at 50.

**What differs is the filter's own input**, and it differs enough to matter:

| | Arm A | Arm B |
|---|---|---|
| Median box range | 31.0 pips | 26.7 pips |
| Days passing the filter | 458 (0.895) | 475 (0.928) |
| Days excluded by the filter | **54** | **37** |

**A one-hour clock shift changes the excluded population by 31%** (54 → 37 days) — the very
population the whole test rests on, and the one whose n was already marginal. `PT-016` §5
named this row in advance:

> *"Arms A and B disagree → The box moves an hour between `D-031` arms and its measured range
> moves with it. Report both; **a filter whose input is timezone-sensitive is a filter with a
> hidden parameter.**"*

**That row fires.** `< 50 pips` is not a well-defined criterion until `A-019` is settled,
because "the Asian range" is not one number until the clock is.

## 8. RULE APPLICATION VERSUS OUTCOME

- **Rule application: fully mechanical, no discretion.** Arm assignment is a function of the
  box range at 03:00; the trigger is a function of one close; direction, stop and target are
  fixed. Nothing was graded because nothing was judged.
- **Days excluded: only for coverage, and counted** (§1a). All 512 included session days are
  in one arm or the other. Days on which the
  trade proxy never triggered (26% / 23%) are counted and reported as a rate, not dropped.
- **Losers retained**, and unresolved positions marked to market at 17:00 rather than
  discarded.
- **No parameter was adjusted.** The 50-pip cut, the 25–50 trigger band, 18/50, the seed and
  the iteration count are exactly as pre-registered.
- **One pre-registration ambiguity, declared:** `PT-016` §3 measure (b) reads *"whether the
  day's extreme forms beyond the box"*. On this corpus the "either edge" form is **1.000 by
  construction** — `BT_V04_0001` §4 found all 512 days cleared at least one edge — so it is
  reported alongside the informative per-edge and both-edge forms rather than alone. No
  threshold or comparator moved; a tautology was labelled as one.

## 9. WHAT THIS OBSERVATION DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**Does:**
- **The `< 50 pip` filter admits ~90% of GBP/USD days in W-B** (0.895 / 0.928) — it is a rule
  about outliers, not a strategy filter.
- **It produces no separation on any pre-registered outcome measure**, and the point
  estimates on hit rate and expectancy run **against** the claim on both `D-031` arms.
- **A split at the sample's own median — a number with no doctrinal claim — separates
  outcomes better and in the claimed direction.** So 50 is not special.
- Neither arm clears its own N1 at any percentile above 81.4.
- The only significant difference (normalised excursion, p = 0.000 / 0.013) is **mechanical**.
- **The filter's input is timezone-sensitive**: the excluded population is 54 days on Arm A
  and 37 on Arm B.

**Does not:**
- Authorise `< 50 pips` as a course rule, or as a `12_MASTER_SPEC` / `13_MACHINE_SPEC` entry.
  **§1a forbids that regardless of what was found, and nothing favourable was found.**
- Establish that large boxes are *better*. The arm-H point estimates are higher and every
  interval overlaps; Arm B's arm-H trade proxy is `SAMPLE INSUFFICIENT` at 24 resolved trades.
- Test the Market Maker Method entry rule (`A-007`, `A-011`, `A-039` all undefined).
- Close `C-004`, `A-006`, `A-019` or any other record.
- Generalise beyond GBP/USD, 2014-01-05 → 2015-12-31, and the V02 printed box.
