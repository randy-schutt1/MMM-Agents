## §1 — HEADLINE

**Two claims tested. Neither returns a clean verdict, and the reasons are different and both worth
having.**

⭐⭐ **CLAIM `W` (the wick) SPLITS INTO ITS TWO LEGS, AND THE SPLIT IS THE RESULT.**

| Leg | Real | Shuffled null | Diff |
|---|---|---|---|
| `O1` median wick **share of range** | `0.542`–`0.555` | `0.541`–`0.556` | **−0.006 to +0.013** — *inside `±0.05` in all four cells* |
| `O2` fraction of days with **both wicks ≥ 5 pips** | `0.799`–`0.854` | `0.718`–`0.738` | **+0.081 to +0.116** — *above `+0.05` in all four cells* |

> **The SIZE of a daily wick is indistinguishable from a shuffled version of the same day. The
> PRESENCE of a wick on both ends is not.** Real GBP/USD days end away from both extremes about 9
> percentage points more often than a volatility-matched random reordering of their own minutes.

⚠ **The pre-registered decision rule has no cell for this, and the result fell into the gap.**
`§6` of `PT-045` defined `SUPPORTED` (both legs `> +0.05`), `NOT SUPPORTED` (both within `±0.05`),
`CONTRADICTED` (either `< −0.05`) and `INDETERMINATE` (the legs *"disagree in sign"*). **These legs
do not disagree in sign; one is null and one is positive.** The runner returns `INDETERMINATE` in
all four cells because that is the only remaining branch. **The pre-registration governs and is not
edited** (`COMMON_PROTOCOL.md` §9 rule 7). **This is a defect in my own decision table, reported
rather than repaired after the fact.**

⚠ **CLAIM `S` (the swing): the number is roughly right and the CONDITIONING adds nothing.**

## §2a — CLASSIFICATION (`BACKTEST_EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md`)

### Classification: **EVIDENTIAL for `W`, ⚠ WEAK for `S`**

**`W` is EVIDENTIAL.** `n` is 896–2174 days per cell; the comparator is a null built from **each
day's own minute returns**, so volatility, return distribution and length are matched exactly; the
effect on `O2` is `+0.08`–`+0.12` in **four of four** cells across two disjoint windows and two
arms. **The direction and rough size replicate.**

**`S` is WEAK and is labelled as such.** `n` is 107–288 reversal events. The margins are within
`±0.03`, which is inside what a `n = 107` cell can resolve, and **the arms disagree on `W-E`**.
**No verdict on `S` should be relied on.**

### Intervals

`O6`'s bootstrap intervals (`mmm_lib.boot_ci`, 2,000 resamples, fixed seed) exclude zero in all four
cells. ⚠ **`§5`'s note applies and it is important: `O6` is partly tautological** — see `§6c`.

`O2`'s difference, as a Wilson interval on the real rate against the pooled shuffled rate, is
comfortably separated in every cell (`n ≥ 779` real against `≥ 15,580` shuffled replicates). **The
`O2` effect is not a sampling artefact.**

### The baseline question, answered explicitly

**What would a coin do?** That is precisely what `N1` is. A shuffled day is a random reordering of
the same day's real minute moves. **It produces a median wick share of `0.54`, essentially identical
to the real one.** ⭐ **So more than half of a GBP/USD daily bar's range being "wick" is not a fact
about market makers; it is a fact about paths.** The only part that is *not* the coin is that real
days more reliably leave a **non-trivial wick on both ends at once**.

## §4 — WHAT THE `O2` RESULT ACTUALLY SAYS ABOUT V17

V17's claim is that the wick **is** the end-of-cycle consolidation: *"the wick on the daily candle
represents the consolidation off of the high or off of the low To end the cycle for 24 hours."*

**The measurement decomposes that into two questions and answers them differently.**

1. **"Is a daily bar mostly wick?"** Yes — about 54% of its range. **And so is a shuffled day.**
   This part of the claim describes path geometry and needs no market maker.
2. **"Does price reliably retreat from BOTH extremes before the close?"** ⭐ **Yes, and more often
   than the shuffle** — `0.80`–`0.85` against `0.72`–`0.74`. **This is the part of V17's claim that
   survives a matched null.**

⚠ **It does NOT show the retreat is "consolidation", and it does not show it happens at the END of
the day.** `PT-045` `§8` said so before the run and it is restated here unchanged: a `SUPPORTED`
result on geometry is not a result on mechanism. **A test that timed the retreat would be a
different test and this project has not run one.**

## §6 — DISAGREEMENTS AND CORRECTIONS, REPORTED RATHER THAN EDITED AWAY

### §6a — ⚠⚠ THE FIRST EXECUTION'S CLAIM-`S` FIGURES ARE AN ARTEFACT, AND THEY ARE RETAINED

`PT-045` §3a required, for the swing test, that days `R−3 … R+4` be *"all present and consecutive"*.
**The runner's first execution implemented "consecutive" as CONSECUTIVE CALENDAR DAYS.**

⚠⚠ **FX has no Saturday or Sunday session.** Under that reading every window spanning a weekend
fails, which:

* dropped **555–1,387 windows per cell**, keeping only **12–76**;
* **truncated every run at the following weekend**, which forces `P(run ≥ 3) = 0` **mechanically**;
* **forced the `N3` control to `0.0` as well — and that is the tell.** A control that returns exactly
  zero in all four cells is not measuring anything.

### §6b — ⚠ A REPRODUCIBILITY DEFECT IN MY OWN RUNNER, FOUND AND FIXED

The shuffle seed was written as `SEED + hash(label) % 9973`. **Python salts `str.__hash__` per
process**, so `O3` was **not reproducible across runs** — the first two executions returned
shuffled medians differing in the third decimal (`0.5481` vs `0.5508` for the same cell).
`COMMON_PROTOCOL.md` §2 requires that re-running reproduces every figure exactly, **seed included**.

**Fixed** to a fixed per-label/per-arm offset table, and **verified**: two independent full
executions of the corrected runner are **byte-identical**.

⚠ **The defect never touched a verdict** — the drift was `< 0.003` on `O3` against a `0.05`
threshold — **but it would have made this test irreproducible, which is worse than being wrong.**

### §6c — ⚠ `O6` IS PARTLY TAUTOLOGICAL AS CONSTRUCTED, AND IT IS NOT AN EDGE

`O6` measures `close(R−1) → close(R+2)` signed by `sign(R)`. **`R`'s own move is inside that window
and its sign is `sign(R)` by definition**, so a positive `O6` is guaranteed to be biased upward.
The `+49` to `+80` pip means and their intervals are therefore **descriptive of a three-day swing
that includes its own first day, not evidence of a tradeable continuation.**

**This is disclosed rather than corrected** because `PT-045` §5 defined `O6` as *"cumulative move
over `R … R+2`"* and this is a faithful implementation of that phrase. **A future test wanting a
continuation edge must measure `close(R) → close(R+2)` and must pre-register it as such.**

### §6d — WHAT THE RUNNER DOES THAT THE PRE-REGISTRATION DID NOT SPECIFY

* `SHUFFLE_ITER = 20` replicates per day. `PT-045` §5a said *"`ITERATIONS` and `SEED` are
  `mmm_lib`'s, unchanged"* and `mmm_lib.ITERATIONS` governs the bootstrap, not the shuffle. **20 is
  the runner's choice**, giving 15,580–43,480 shuffled days per cell. Disclosed.
* **`O6`'s adjacency is evaluated under `READING 2` semantics in both readings.** It is a secondary
  measure and enters no verdict; disclosed for completeness.
