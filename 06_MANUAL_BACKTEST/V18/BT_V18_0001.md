**Establishes:** under the most natural fixed-clock three-session partition available, on 908
complete GBP/USD session days of DEVELOPMENT data, **V18's most-repeated rule has no detectable
edge, and its implied run-length signature (mode 2) is absent — the observed mode is 1 and length-2
runs occur at 99.7% of the coin-flip rate.** The result is robust to the one invented boundary.

**Does NOT establish** — and the pre-registration said so in advance:

1. **That V18 is wrong.** The lesson states the rule about *the dealer's* sessions and **explicitly
   refuses to fix which session corrects** (`[00:09:27]`). A fixed clock partition is a substitution
   this test had to make and the lesson does not endorse.
2. **Anything about the safety trade**, the `W V V M` labelling, or the 2-hour rule. Those need
   formation recognition V18 never supplies.
3. **Anything about a different instrument or timeframe.** GBP/USD M15 only.

**What it does do is constrain CODING**, which is the only reason this project runs tests: **the
two-sessions-then-corrective rule must not be coded as a directional prior.** It is not one.
