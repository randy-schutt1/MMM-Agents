## §3 — ⚠️⚠️ THE BASELINE, AND WHY IT GUTS THE VERDICT

`N1` (`PT-048` §4): windows of **the same lengths**, drawn at random from the same days and hours,
with **no leg condition**, measured identically.

| | median `P1` | in `[20, 25]` |
|---|---|---|
| **Trend legs** (primary cell) | **22.45** | **17.1 %** |
| ⭐ **`N1` matched random windows** | **23.00** | **18.9 %** |

⛔⛔ **THE NULL PRODUCES THE SAME NUMBER — AND VERY SLIGHTLY MORE OF IT.**

**Reading, stated flatly:** *"20 to 25 pips"* is a correct description of **the typical counter-swing
on GBP/USD M15 in 2014–2015**. It is **not** shown to be a property of *trend legs*, of *market-maker
profit-booking*, or of anything the lesson attributes it to. **Any 6-plus-bar window of this
instrument has pullbacks of about this size, trend or no trend.**

⭐ **This does not make V20 wrong.** The instructor said the trend move *contains* 20–25 pip
pullbacks, and it does. ⛔ **It makes the observation non-diagnostic**: it cannot separate his
explanation from *"this is what GBP/USD does."*

### §3a — ⚠️⚠️ THE DEFECT IN MY OWN PRE-REGISTRATION, REPORTED AS A DEFECT

**`PT-048` §4 defined `N1` and stated its logic correctly** — *"The claim is that trend legs have a

⛔ **And then §5's decision rule never mentioned `N1`, and `N3` never guarded on it.** **I named the

**A pre-registration that had done its job would have required the trend-leg median to differ from
`N1`'s** before awarding anything. **This one awards `CONFIRMED` on a band-membership test that the
null also passes.** ⚠️ **The verdict stands as the rule produced it — `COMMON_PROTOCOL.md` §9 rule 7
means the pre-registration is not edited and not re-scored after the fact — but the rule was
inadequate and the inadequacy is mine.**

⭐ **This is the same class of defect `REVIEW_INDEX.md` item 289 charged against `PT-047`:** five
weaknesses declared in advance, and the most consequential one missed. **`PT-048` §2 declared five
and missed this one. Recorded so the next pre-registration in this project makes the baseline a

## §5 — WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**It establishes:** on GBP/USD M15 over 2014–2015 development data, on both `D-031` arms and both
windows, **the median counter-swing inside a ≥ 40-pip directional leg is 20.7–23.5 pips**, with the
primary cell's interval inside V20's stated band — **and the same statistic on matched random
windows is 23.0 pips.**

**It does NOT establish:**

* ⛔ **that the `20–25` band is diagnostic of anything.** §3. **This is the headline limitation.**
* ⚠️ **that pullbacks are usually 20–25 pips.** Only **17.1 %** are; the IQR is 16.4–32.8.
* ⚠️ **anything about the `3 levels of move`.** Not tested — *"level"* is undefined (`A-004`).
* ⚠️ **anything about *"slow and steady"***, `L3`, the outside structure, or the one-third entry.
  All blocked or unmeasurable (`A-136`, `A-137`, `A-138`, `D-030`).
* ⚠️ **that this is tradeable.** No spread, slippage, cost or execution. `D-006`.
* ⚠️ **anything out of sample.** The `D-035` holdout was not touched, and **`W-B` CONTAINS `W-A`** —
  a wider-window replication, not an independent one. ⭐ **`PT-048` §3 said so in advance**, which is
  the wording defect `BT_V19_0001.md` §6 had to correct in `PT-047`.

**One, and it is a scope reduction the pre-registration did not authorise:**

⚠️ **`PT-048` §4 specifies `N1` at *"10,000 iterations"*. The runner draws `min(ITERS, 2000)` = 2000
random windows** (each yielding several `P1` values, for `n = 5601`). **The reduction was made for
runtime and is a departure from the committed spec.** ⛔ **The pre-registration is not edited.**

than the 0.55-pip gap that would need to close for the null to separate. **But it is a divergence
and it is reported rather than absorbed.**

**No other disagreement.** `PIVOT_K = 3`, `LEG_MIN_PIPS = 40`, `LEG_MIN_BARS = 6`, `BAND = (20, 25)`,
the three measures, `P1` as primary, the four `N3` conditions and the four-branch decision rule are
all the values fixed in `PT-048` before the run.
