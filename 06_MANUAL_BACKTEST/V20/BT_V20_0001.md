### §2a — ⚠️⚠️ THE CORRECTION: WHY THIS TABLE DIFFERS FROM THE ONE FILED ON 2026-08-15

**V20 R1 `M1` / `REVIEW_INDEX.md` item 332, and the finding is correct.**

`PT-048` §3.1 defines a swing pivot as *"a bar whose high is the maximum (or low the minimum) of the
**`±3`** bars around it"*, and `P1` as the retracement at *"each **interior counter-swing** inside
the leg"*. ⛔ **`run_pt048.py` applied `PIVOT_K = 3` to the leg endpoints and used a hardcoded `±1`
for the interior counter-swings** — so every `P1` value in the original table came from a scale the
pre-registration does not specify.

**A `±1` local minimum is not a swing; on M15 it is roughly every third bar.**

⭐ **The runner has been corrected to use `PIVOT_K` for both**, and the swing-scale sensitivity is
now **published on every run** rather than left invisible:

| interior swing at | `n` | median `P1` | boot 95 % | in band? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `k = 1` — the old runner's hardcoded value | 2506 | **22.45** | `[22.00, 23.20]` | ✅ |
| `k = 2` | 1200 | 26.80 | `[25.90, 27.60]` | ❌ |
| ⭐ **`k = 3` — `PT-048` §3.1's own definition** | **702** | ⛔ **30.10** | `[28.80, 31.25]` | ❌ |
| `k = 4` | 440 | 33.10 | `[31.30, 34.90]` | ❌ |

⛔⛔ **THE CHOICE WAS VERDICT-DETERMINING AND IT RAN TOWARD THE CLAIMED BAND.** `k = 1` was the
**only** scale of the four that put the median inside `[20, 25]`, and by inflating `n` from 702 to
2506 it also narrowed the bootstrap interval enough to satisfy §5's *interval-inside-the-band*
condition. **It manufactured both halves of `CONFIRMED`.**

⚠️⚠️ **AND THE PREVIOUS §6 ASSERTED THERE WAS NO SUCH DIVERGENCE.** It listed `PIVOT_K = 3` among
the values *"fixed in `PT-048` before the run"* under the heading *"No other disagreement"*. **That
sentence was true of the leg constructor and false of the primary measure.** ⭐ **The reviewer's
independent re-implementation returned 30.10 `[28.70, 31.25]`; this corrected runner returns 30.10
`[28.80, 31.25]`. The figures agree to the second decimal.**

⚠️ **In fairness to the original, and the reviewer says it first: `PT-048` §3 does not restate `±3`
inside the `P1` row.** ⛔ **That does not rescue it.** If the interior scale was underspecified it
was a **free convention on the primary measure with a verdict-flipping effect**, and it had to be
declared and its sensitivity shown. **`N3`'s four conditions do not include swing scale — which is
why the sensitivity table above is now permanent output.**

⭐ **This finding is SCALE-INVARIANT and therefore survives `M1` untouched.** At `k = 1` the gap was

**Reading, stated flatly:** whatever the typical counter-swing on GBP/USD M15 is, **it is a property
of the instrument and the swing scale you measure at — not of trend legs, of market-maker
profit-booking, or of anything the lesson attributes it to.**

⚠️ **So the claim fails twice over, on independent grounds:** the magnitude is wrong at the
pre-registered scale (§0), **and** the quantity is non-diagnostic at every scale (here).

## §5 — WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**It establishes:** on GBP/USD M15 over 2014–2015 development data, on both `D-031` arms and both
windows, **the median swing retracement inside a ≥ 40-pip directional leg, measured at the
pre-registered `±3` swing scale, is 27.1–31.0 pips** — **above** V20's stated `20–25` band, with the
primary cell's interval excluding it — **and the same statistic on matched random windows is
29.15 pips.**

**It does NOT establish:**

* ⛔ **that V20's `20 to 25` figure is wrong about anything the instructor can see.** ⚠️ **The
  measured quantity depends on the swing scale** — `k = 1` gives 22.45, `k = 3` gives 30.10 (§2a) —
  **and the lesson does not specify a scale at all.** **A `REFUTED` here refutes the claim AS
  OPERATIONALISED BY `PT-048` §3, which is a convention this test declared and the lesson never
  endorsed.** This is `PT-048` §6 attack 1, and it lands with full force on the corrected result.
* ⛔ **that the quantity is diagnostic of trend legs.** §3, and that finding is scale-invariant.
* ⚠️ **anything about the `3 levels of move`** — *"level"* undefined (`A-004`).
* ⚠️ **anything about *"slow and steady"***, `L3`, the outside structure, or the one-third entry.
* ⚠️ **that this is tradeable.** No spread, slippage, cost or execution. `D-006`.
* ⚠️ **anything out of sample.** The `D-035` holdout was untouched, and **`W-B` CONTAINS `W-A`**.

⭐⭐ **THE HONEST ONE-LINE READING: `PT-048` asked whether a 20–25 pip pullback is characteristic of
trend legs on GBP/USD M15. The answer is NO on both halves — the magnitude is larger at the scale
this test pre-registered, and whatever the magnitude, it is not specific to trend legs.** ⚠️ **And
the magnitude half is only as good as a swing-scale convention the lesson never gave.**
