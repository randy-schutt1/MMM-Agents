## 2. A DEFECT FOUND AND FIXED BEFORE ANY NUMBER WAS READ

The first implementation held the **intraday** boundary labels fixed under N2 and moved only
the week anchor. **That is not N2.** `COMMON_PROTOCOL.md` §5 defines N2 as *"all
session/boundary labels shifted by an offset"* — the whole system moves together. The symptom
that exposed it was an N2 percentile of **exactly 46.8 at all three bands**, which is not a
coincidence a correct implementation produces.

Fixing it changed the N2 median at ±30 from 0.3394 to **0.2737** and the reported percentile
from 46.8 to **74.4**. **No threshold moved and the verdict did not change**, but the wrong
number would have been on the record.

**The fix surfaced a second thing that is not a bug and does matter**, and it is what §3a
exists to catch:

| Null | What moves | Covered fraction at ±30 |
|---|---|---|
| rule arm | nothing | **0.2521** |
| **N2** | labels **and** week anchor, rigidly | 0.2522 — **the same** |
| **N3** | **only** the week anchor | **0.2625 — larger** |

**In reality the week open and week close both land on 17:00, which is already a day
boundary and already the New York session close.** The two week boundaries therefore add
**almost no new covered area**. Shift the week anchor off 17:00, as N3 does by construction,
and they start covering fresh minutes — so **N3's raw share is inflated for a purely
arithmetic reason.** Comparing raw shares across anchorings that cover different amounts of
the week measures the coverage, not the clock.

**Both comparisons are therefore reported below**, and where they disagree the
**§3a-corrected ratio (observed ÷ covered) governs** — that is the correction the
pre-registration demands in its own words: *"A finding of '41% of extremes fall in the bands'
against an expected 38% is a null result, and must be written as one."*

**The one cell that moves toward significance is Arm A at ±60 against N2 (percentile 92.9)** —

## 8. WHAT THIS OBSERVATION DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**Does:**
- **The six printed boundaries carry no detectable timing information for GBP/USD weekly
  extremes over 180 trading weeks, on either `D-031` arm.** Observed ratio 1.311 / 1.247 at
  the pre-registered band; N2 percentile 76.6 / 76.8; N3 percentile **20.8 / 29.0**.
- **Establishes the sharper negative that N3 was pre-registered to deliver.** Two of the six
  boundaries *are* the week anchor, and the real anchor does **not** outperform a randomly
  shifted one — it slightly underperforms it, on both arms, at all three bands.
- **Documents an arithmetic trap that would have manufactured a positive result:** because the
  real week boundaries coincide with 17:00, they add almost no covered area, while a shifted
  anchor does. The raw-share comparison against N3 reads as percentile 8.3; the
  coverage-corrected one reads as 20.8. **Neither clears, but only one of them is honest.**
- Confirms `PT-025` §3d's advance prediction that **four of six per-boundary cells would fall
  below n = 30**, including both week boundaries.

**Does not:**
- Test the trap move (`A-002`), any pattern, or any entry.
- Support the **week-boundaries-only** ratio of 3.97 as a finding — **n = 18, descriptive
  only** (§4).
- Support Arm A's ±60/N2 percentile of 92.9 as a finding — it is a non-pre-registered band on
  one arm and one null, and the same cell's N3 percentile is 11.6.
- Close `A-002`, `A-019`, `C-001`, `I-010` Q1 or Q2.
- Say anything about **daily** extremes — that is `PT-002`'s W-A arm, reported in
  `BT_V01_0001`, which reaches the same verdict by a different route.
- Generalise beyond GBP/USD and 2013-01-06 → 2016-06-30. **The 2017 regime and the October
  2016 flash crash are in HOLDOUT and were not opened.**
