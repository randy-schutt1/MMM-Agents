# BT_V14_0001 — "The lock": does a one-hour hold make it the day's extreme?

```text
CLASSIFICATION:  EVIDENTIAL
VERDICT:         NOT SUPPORTED
```

**The distance is there and the premise is not.** The course's own premise —
*"after that number is a lock for one hour… **it's the high of the day**"* — is **wrong about
two days in three**, and the trade built on it misses its pre-registered boundary in both
`D-031` arms while still beating matched-random entry decisively.

### 2a. The arm asymmetry in exclusions is explained, not a defect

**68 of arm B's 70 `empty leg` exclusions are Saturdays** — session-day shells the DST shift
creates, correctly carrying no candidate. **This is the same class V13 R1 identified in `PT-041`
and correctly declined to treat as a defect.** The remaining two (`2014-06-02` Monday,
`2015-01-01` New Year's Day) and all three `post-T0 <90%` days (`2014-04-18` Good Friday,
`2014-12-24`, `2015-12-24`) are **shared by both arms**. Final `n` is 471 vs 467 — comparable.

### 2b. ⭐ THE FIRST FINDING IS IN THE POPULATION TABLE, NOT THE OUTCOMES

**`F1` retains 92% of session days, and every single retained day both extended and locked.**

- **`no extension` = 0 in both arms.** A ≤50-pip 8-hour range is exceeded in the following
  16 hours on **every one of 938 arm-days**.
- **`no lock` = 0 in both arms**, which makes **`N4` degenerate — it has `n = 0` and returns
  `nan`.** Reported as a null, not quietly dropped.

**Consequence, and it is the most useful thing this test found:** *"wait for the lock"* **does not
select days.** Over a 16-hour window a 60-minute no-new-extreme gap always eventually occurs, so
the rule chooses **a time within the day**, never **whether to trade the day at all**. The filtering
work the drill appears to do is done entirely by `F1`, and `F1` discards 8% of days.

⚠️ **`N4`'s emptiness is a pre-registration defect, disclosed here rather than smoothed over.**
`PT-042` §5a called `N4` *"the comparison that matters"* — it isolates what the one-hour hold adds
over merely extending. **That comparison cannot be made**, because the contrast class is empty.
**Nothing in the verdict rests on `N4`**; `O1` and `O2` are pure rule-arm measurements against
boundaries fixed in advance.

## 5. ⭐ WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED — THE DISTANCE IS REAL, THE PREMISE IS NOT

**This is the same shape `PT-041` found, arrived at independently.**

**`O4` median MFE is 40 pips.** After the lock, price moves **40 pips in the trade's favour, at the
median, on a typical day.** The course's *"aim for 30 to 50 pips"* is **not a fantasy — it is
roughly the right size for the move that is there.**

**And `O2` is only 0.44–0.46, because the 5-pip stop is hit first more often than the 30-pip target
is reached.** The trade fails not because the move is absent but because **the stop is too tight
for the level it is attached to.** `O3` sharpens this: half the trades that reach 30 go on to reach
50 (0.27–0.30 against 0.44–0.46).

**`O1` is the real casualty and it is not close.** The premise is that a one-hour hold **makes** the
level the day's extreme. **It survives to the 17:00 close on 30–35% of days.** The level is
exceeded later in the session about **two days in three**.

### 5a. The `A-090` sensitivity runs the counter-intuitive way, and it is explicable

**`S1` — the better entry price — performs WORSE**: `O2 | filled` = 0.3386 / 0.3249 against the
market-entry arm's 0.4607 / 0.4433, on an 81–84% fill rate.

**The mechanism is selection, and it points the same way as `O1`.** A limit 10 pips inside `L` only
fills when price comes **back toward the level** — and days on which the level is retested are
disproportionately days on which it eventually **breaks**. **Waiting for a better price selects for
the trades that lose.** Recorded because it was pre-registered as a sensitivity and would otherwise
be an unreported null.

## 6. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SHOW

**Does:**

- The course's stated premise is **measurably wrong on GBP/USD in 2014–2015** under the six
  computable steps, by a wide margin, in both timezone arms, on `n ≈ 470` per arm.
- The rule nonetheless carries **real signal against matched-random entry** — `O2` doubles it and
  sits at the 100th percentile of the control distribution. **A `NOT SUPPORTED` verdict is not the
  same as "there is nothing here"**, and the record says both.
- **The lock does not select days.** It selects a time. That was not knowable before the run.

**Does NOT:**

- ❌ **Not a P&L result.** No spread, commission or slippage. **A 5-pip stop sits inside a realistic
  2012 GBP/USD spread band**, so the live version of this drill would fare **worse**, not better.
- ❌ **Not a test of the method as taught.** Step 2 (*"trading in the middle of the range"*) is
  **excluded** under `D-030` (`A-089`), so the population is **larger and less selective** than the
  course intends. **Every figure here is a LOWER BOUND**, declared in `PT-042` §1a before the run.
  ⚠️ **It is entirely possible that the missing filter is what makes the drill work**, and this test
  cannot say otherwise.
- ❌ **Not evidence about "the majors".** One instrument; the cross-sectional pick `[00:38:15]`
  *"narrow it down to two or three pairs"* is part of the method and is untested.
- ❌ **Nothing about `A-077`'s WEEKLY lock.** `A-094` records that V14's one hour and V10's
  *"15, 16 hours"* are figures for **different objects**. This result **must not** be carried onto
  V10's safety trade.
- ❌ **Not a refutation of the drill's stated PURPOSE.** `[00:37:12]` *"a drill **to help you
  understand the candles**"*. **A pedagogical exercise is not falsified by its hit rate**, and
  `V14_INTERPRETATION.md` Q4 records that the lesson calls it both a drill and tradeable.
- ❌ **Does not make the 5-pip stop or 30-pip target doctrine.** They appear in **no** file under
  `12_MASTER_SPEC/` or `13_MACHINE_SPEC/`. `A-082` fence held.

1. `F2` excluded (`A-089`) — population larger and less selective than taught.
2. GBP/USD only (`D-007`).
3. **M1 as a proxy for a tick board** — biases locks **earlier** than a real board would show.
4. Entry offset ambiguous (`A-090`) — handled by a primary + a declared sensitivity.
5. `mmm_lib.SEED` is one fixed constant; **`REVIEW_INDEX.md` item 113 is inherited and not
   discharged.**
6. No sensitivity on the 60-minute lock itself — **deliberately.** `[00:46:05]` offers
   *"one hour, one and a half hours, ninety minutes"*. **The printed slide says `1 hour` and that
   is what was tested. A post-hoc sweep would be threshold-shopping** and is forbidden to this
   session; it would be a new `PT` number.
7. ⭐ **LEARNED IN THE RUN — `N4` is degenerate (`n = 0`).** The contrast `PT-042` §5a called *"the
   comparison that matters"* does not exist. See §2b.
8. ⭐ **LEARNED IN THE RUN — `N1`'s `O1` construction was underspecified and is not comparable.**
