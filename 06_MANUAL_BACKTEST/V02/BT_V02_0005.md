## 2. THE THIRD ARM IS DEGENERATE BY CONSTRUCTION — AND THAT IS ITSELF THE FINDING

`PT-005` §4 calls the entry-relative grid *"what makes this test worth running"*:

> *"Without it, a stop-out spike at 09:30 is indistinguishable from a stop-out spike at
> **entry + 90 minutes**, and the instructor's claim is specifically about the clock."*

**With a fixed 08:00 entry the two grids cannot differ.** Every trade enters at exactly 08:15,
so wall-clock stop-out time **= 08:15 + elapsed**, deterministically. The two histograms are
an affine relabelling of one another and carry identical information — visible in §3, where
the entry-relative row is the wall-clock row with different labels.

> **A fixed entry time cannot separate "09:30 matters" from "entry + 75 minutes matters".**
> The pre-registration's own design contains this, and it is reported rather than patched:
> **nothing was approximated and no substitute arm was invented** (`D-030`). The separation is
> supplied instead by **N1 and N2, which both vary the entry hour** — and both were
> pre-registered for this test.

## 4. THE N1 BASELINE IS BIASED BY ITS OWN CONSTRUCTION, AND THE 96.3 / 100.0 FIGURES MUST NOT BE QUOTED

N1 as pre-registered draws the entry bar **at random from 08:00–12:00**. **An entry after
10:00 can never produce a stop-out in the 09:30–10:00 window** — the window is already past.
N1 therefore **mechanically understates** the share of stop-outs landing there, and the rule
arm's percentile against it is inflated.

| | Arm A LONG | Arm B LONG |
|---|---|---|
| Percentile within **N1** (biased) | 96.3 | 100.0 |
| Percentile within **N2** (unbiased) | **52.1** | **93.5** |

> **N2 is the comparator that answers the question**, because a circular clock shift moves the
> entry *and* the event together and so preserves the 75-minute runway the rule arm enjoys.
> **The N1 percentiles are reported for completeness and are not the result.** Quoting the
> 96.3 or the 100.0 as support would be reporting an artifact of the null's construction.
>
> This is recorded rather than corrected: **N1 is what `PT-005` §4 pre-registered**, and it is
> not this session's to change (`D-027`). The defect is disclosed and the pre-registered N2 is
> used for the verdict.

## 5. `D-031` — THE SHARPEST ARM DIVERGENCE IN THE BATCH, AND `PT-005` §5 NAMED IT IN ADVANCE

> *"Arm A spikes at 09:30 and arm B at 08:30 (or vice versa) → **The sharpest possible
> `D-031` result — a one-hour displacement is exactly what separates the arms.** Report both;
> conclude nothing about `A-019`, which stays open."*

**That row fires, in the "vice versa" form.**

| | Arm A | Arm B |
|---|---|---|
| Share of stop-outs in 09:30–10:00, LONG | **0.104** | **0.194** |
| Percentile within N2, LONG | **52.1** | **93.5** |
| Share, SHORT | 0.093 | 0.169 |
| Percentile within N2, SHORT | 43.8 | 85.0 |

**The mechanism is identifiable and it is not noise.** Arm A is fixed `UTC−5`; Arm B is New
York local. During US DST — two-thirds of the year — a New-York-local 09:30 event is stamped
**08:30** on Arm A. So:

- **Arm A's 08:30–09:00 bin blends two different events**: the New York 09:30 open during DST
  months, *and* the 08:30 US data release during standard months. They collide in one bin.
- **Arm B separates them**: the 08:30 release stays at 08:30 all year, the 09:30 open stays at
  09:30 all year. Arm B's LONG histogram is correspondingly **bimodal** — 60 at 08:30–09:00
  and 60 at 09:30–10:00.

> **The instructor recorded the bootcamp 2012-03-18 → 2012-06-17, entirely inside US daylight
> saving** (`D-031`'s own arithmetic in `DECISIONS.md`). **Arm B is therefore the arm that
> reproduces his wall clock during the period he was speaking** — and on that arm the claim
> looks materially better: percentile **93.5 / 85.0** against **52.1 / 43.8**.
>
> **It still does not clear the pre-registered bar.** Neither direction on either arm exceeds
> the 95th percentile of N2. **The verdict is `INDISTINGUISHABLE FROM THE NULL`**, and
> selecting Arm B because it flatters the claim would be `E09` + `E24` and is exactly what
> `D-031` forbids.
>
> **And `PT-005` §5 is explicit that this concludes nothing about `A-019`, which stays open.**

## 7. RULE APPLICATION VERSUS OUTCOME

- **Rule application: fully mechanical, zero discretion.** Entry time, stop, target and both
  direction arms are fixed. Every trading day is taken; no filter of any kind was applied.
- **Days excluded: only for coverage, counted and labelled** (§0), and the included sets are

- **Losers retained**: 288/324/310/326 stop-outs are the entire subject of Measure 1, and
  positions still open at 17:00 are counted.
- **N1 and N2 were computed and printed before the observed histogram** (`PT-005` §7 step 3),
  and the wall-clock and entry-relative histograms were produced in the same pass (step 4).
- **No parameter was adjusted.** No bin, window, direction or arm was selected after a result.

## 8. WHAT THIS OBSERVATION DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**Does:**
- **The stop-out peak against an 08:00 position is 08:30–09:00, not 09:30–10:00, on both arms
  and both directions.**
- **The claim is `INDISTINGUISHABLE FROM THE NULL`**: nothing clears the 95th percentile of
  N2 — 52.1 / 43.8 (Arm A) and 93.5 / 85.0 (Arm B).
- **The sharpest `D-031` divergence in the batch**, pre-named by `PT-005` §5: the 09:30–10:00
  share nearly doubles between arms (0.104 → 0.194), with an identifiable mechanism — Arm A
  blends the DST-months NY open with the standard-months data release into one bin; Arm B
  separates them.
- **Arm B — the arm reproducing the instructor's own 2012 wall clock — leans substantially
  toward the claim without reaching the bar.**
- **The pre-registered N1 baseline is biased by construction** (late entries cannot stop out
  at 09:30) and its 96.3 / 100.0 percentiles are artifacts, not evidence.
- **The pre-registered entry-relative arm is degenerate** with a fixed entry time and cannot
  do the job §4 assigned it.
- **09:30–10:00 is never the peak MAE slot** on any arm or direction.

**Does not:**
- Support *"Forex Factory is clueless"* or refute it — the pre-registered test returns neither.
- Say anything about the Market Maker Method: the 08:00 entry is a measurement device
  (`PT-005` §6), and its +2.51 / −1.28 / +1.02 / −1.13 pip expectancies are not results about
  the method.
- Close `A-019`, which `PT-005` §5 states explicitly stays open.
- Identify the cause of the 08:30 cluster. No economic calendar exists in this corpus.
- Generalise beyond GBP/USD and 2014-01-05 → 2015-12-31.
