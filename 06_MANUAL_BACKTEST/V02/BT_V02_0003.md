### Two declared readings

1. **"Within 30 minutes of the anchor"** (Metric 2) is taken as *either side* of the anchor
   instant — session slots {0, 1} (first 30 min) or {94, 95} (last 30 min).
2. **N2 and the second baseline are the same object at different resolutions.** A circular
   clock shift of a 24-hour anchoring metric *is* the anchor sweep at 15-minute granularity.
   That is a property of this test's design, not a defect, and it is stated rather than
   glossed. Both are reported.

### C-6 completeness, general form

An anchored day enters only if **all 96** of its fifteen-minute buckets exist. Applied
identically to every anchor and to every N2 draw. `QA_REPORT.txt` C8's requirement — that
any flagged session have an explicit pre-registered disposition — is discharged mechanically
by this rule: the four W-A sessions C8 flags (2015-01-01, 2015-12-24, 2015-12-25 and, at the
window edge, 2016-01-01) cannot form a complete 24-hour block and are **excluded and counted**.

## 2. A STRUCTURAL CONFOUND IN THE PRE-REGISTERED SWEEP, FOUND BEFORE ANY RANKING

The raw sweep keeps **256 anchored days at 17:00** and **~206 at every other anchor** (Arm A).

**Cause.** 17:00 is the **only anchor aligned with the market's own weekly open and close**. A
24-hour block anchored at 17:00 is exactly one trading session, so Sunday 17:00 → Monday 17:00
is complete, and Friday 17:00 → Saturday 17:00 contains no bars at all (it is not a block
rather than a broken one). **Every other anchor loses all 52 Sunday starts and ~50 Friday
starts to the weekend gap.** Measured directly:

```text
anchor 00:00   blocks 310   complete 206   dropped 104   (50 Fri starts, 52 Sun starts, 2 other)
anchor 17:00   blocks 258   complete 256   dropped   2
```

> **The raw ranking therefore compares 17:00's 256 days against 00:00's 206 *different* days,
> and 17:00's advantage is a property of clock alignment, not of price behaviour.** Left
> unhandled this would have been a favourable-looking artifact for the very claim under test.

**Handling.** A **matched-basis sweep** restricts *every* anchor to **Mon–Thu block starts**.
**No pre-registered threshold, window, metric or comparator is changed** — the pre-registered
comparison is made valid. **Both sweeps are reported and neither replaces the other.**

**Residual, disclosed:** on the matched basis, anchors 18:00–23:00 retain only 154 days,
because their Thursday-start block runs past the Friday 17:00 close. Anchors 00:00–17:00
retain 204–206 and are directly comparable — which covers the comparison that matters.

> ### **`PT-003` §5 row 1 fires: "17:00 ranks mid-pack among 24 anchors → The printed reset is not visible in GBP/USD price at this sample. Report prominently."**
>
> **Reported prominently.** Across three metrics, two arms and two bases — **twelve rankings**
> — 17:00 reaches the top third **once** (M3, matched basis, Arm B, rank 6). It ranks 16th–19th
> of 24 on M1 in all four cells and 17th–18th on M2 in all four cells. **No percentile within
> N2 reaches 78, let alone 95.**
>
> `PT-003` §5 row 2 — *"17:00 ranks top-3 on all three metrics → strong support"* — **does not
> fire on any arm or basis.** Row 3 — *"top-3 on one metric only → report all three, do not
> headline the one that worked"* — does not fire either; 17:00 is not top-3 anywhere.

**What the sweep does show is that 09:00 is the interesting hour, not 17:00.** It is the
maximum of both M1 (61.96 bars) and M2 (0.5194) on Arm A. That is **not** a claim that 09:00
is the true day boundary — a day anchored at 09:00 straddles the London/New-York activity
peak, which is a sufficient mechanical explanation, and no such anchor is pre-registered or
adopted (`D-010`).

### 4a. Metric 3 is basis-sensitive, and that is worth its own line

**M3's rank for 17:00 moves from 22/24 (raw) to 8/24 (matched) on Arm A** — from near-worst to
above-median — with a percentile shift of 9.4 → 67.3. **The 52 Sunday-start blocks that only
17:00 keeps are unusually evenly-spread days** (a Sunday evening plus a Monday), which drags
17:00's mean Gini down on the raw basis.

**This is exactly the confound §2 identified, and it moves a metric by 14 rank positions.**
Had only the raw sweep been run, M3 would have been reported as 17:00's *worst* result; had
only the matched sweep been run, as its *best*. Both are reported and neither is preferred.

## 5. `D-031` — THE ARMS AGREE ON THE VERDICT

M1 and M2 rank 17:00 within two positions of each other across arms on both bases (16–19 and
17–18). The verdict — mid-pack to low, nothing clearing N2 — is identical.

**The arms differ on M3's raw basis** (rank 22 vs 8; percentile 9.4 vs 69.7) and agree on M3's
matched basis (8 vs 6). That is the same day-set sensitivity as §4a: Arm B's DST shift changes
which blocks are complete (**222 anchored days at 17:00 on Arm B against 256 on Arm A, with 70
excluded against 2**), so the raw basis is doubly confounded on Arm B and the matched basis is
the one to read.

> **A `D-031` finding in its own right:** the Arm-B clock is *not* aligned with the corpus's
> fixed 17:00 week open during US DST, so Arm B loses 68 more anchored days at the 17:00 anchor
> than Arm A does. **The "17:00 reset" is not even the same set of days under the two readings
> of the clock**, which is a concrete cost of `A-019` remaining open.

## 7. WHAT THIS OBSERVATION DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**Does:**
- **The printed 17:00 "High / Low Reset" is not visible in GBP/USD price in W-A.** 17:00 ranks
  16th–19th of 24 on M1 and 17th–18th on M2, on both arms and both bases; nothing clears N2.
- **A structural confound favours 17:00 in the raw sweep** — it is the only anchor aligned with
  the market week, keeping 52 Sunday blocks the others lose — and it is neutralised by a
  matched basis without changing any pre-registered quantity.
- **Metric 3 moves 14 rank positions between bases**, so the confound was material.
- **09:00 maximises M1 and M2**, with a mechanical explanation and no adoption.
- **Arm B loses 70 anchored days at the 17:00 anchor against Arm A's 2** — the two clock
  readings do not even measure the same set of days at the reset hour.

**Does not:**
- Test *"The MM Spread Is Set"* — broker spread is invisible to candle data (`PT-003` §6).
- Bear on `A-019`, which stays open, or license any anchor other than the one the project
  already uses.
- License moving this project's daily grid. `PT-003` §5 is explicit that such a move *"is a
  `DECISIONS.md` change, not a finding a backtest gets to make on its own"* — and nothing here
  would support one anyway.
- Establish that 09:00 is a day boundary. It is not pre-registered and is not adopted (`D-010`).
- Generalise beyond GBP/USD and 2015-01-04 → 2015-12-31.
