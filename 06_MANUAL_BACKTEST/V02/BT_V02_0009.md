# BT_V02_0009 — PT-030: is the previous week's extreme a barrier?

> ## ⚠ MANDATORY SCOPE STATEMENT — `PT-030` §6, verbatim
>
> **PT-030 tests whether the previous week's extreme acts as a barrier on GBP/USD.** It is
> **not** a test of "peak formation" (`A-010`) — the previous week's extreme is a measurement,
> and whether the instructor's peak formation coincides with it is undefined and untested here.
> **It resolves nothing in `C-001`** and adopts no day count. It is not a trading test: no
> entry, stop or target is involved.
>
> It **re-issues `PT-012`** onto `W-C′` under `D-035`; `PT-012` is retained, marked and never
> run, and no result here may be reported as `PT-012`'s result.
>
> **Price levels on this corpus are not comparable with the V02–V06 FXCM homework** (`D-036a`);
> only shape and distance claims travel, and **no barrier price here may be reconciled to an
> FXCM-sourced level**. Breaches are **bid-side** (§0b). The **week open is 22:00 UTC**, not
> FXCM's 21:00 UTC.

## 2. THE BASELINES, BUILT FIRST — AND WHY ARM 1 IS THE WHOLE TEST

`PT-030` §4: *"**Distance to a barrier dominates the breach rate** — a barrier 200 pips away
survives longer than one 20 pips away for reasons that have nothing to do with market makers.
Any comparison that does not hold distance constant measures volatility and reports it as
doctrine."*

| Baseline | upper-barrier breach | lower-barrier breach |
|---|---|---|
| **arm 1 — distance-matched sham**, random side | median **0.5000** [0.4551, 0.5393] | median **0.5056** [0.4607, 0.5506] |
| **arm 2 — N3 week-anchor shift** | median 0.4746 [0.4463, 0.4915] | median 0.5480 [0.5112, 0.5706] |
| **arm 3 — donor-week side, matched distance** | median 0.4775 [0.4382, 0.5225] | median 0.5281 [0.4829, 0.5677] |

**On arm 3, one implementation choice is disclosed:** the pre-registration asks for a barrier
*"taken from a different, randomly chosen week, **scaled to the same distance**"*, which fixes
the distance and leaves only the *side* to come from the donor. It is implemented that way —
the donor supplies which side is nearer, this week supplies the distances. **It therefore
differs from arm 1 only in drawing the side from the empirical rather than the uniform
distribution**, and both are reported.

## 3. RESULT — MEASURE 2, THE BREACH RATE

**Identical on both `D-031` arms** (a barrier is a price and a week is the same physical span
in both; no clock label enters any measure).

| | Observed | vs **arm 1** (distance-matched) | vs arm 2 (N3) | vs arm 3 (donor) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **upper barrier** (last week's high) | **0.466 (83/178)** [0.394, 0.540] | **percentile 8.4** | 25.9 | 27.1 |
| **lower barrier** (last week's low) | **0.539 (96/178)** [0.466, 0.611] | **percentile 86.6** | 35.0 | 64.7 |
| both breached | 18 | | | |
| neither breached | 17 | | | |

> ### **First, the absolute claim. V02 states it absolutely — *"they will not go below last week's peak formation"* — and the barrier is breached in about half of all weeks. An absolute claim is refuted by a single counter-example; this is 83 and 96 of them.**
>
> `V02_INTERPRETATION.md` `G10` records the claim as *"unfalsifiable as presented"* because the
> lesson shows no case where the dealer did cross. **This test supplies the missing
> denominator, and the answer is that crossing is ordinary.**
>
> ### **Second, the statistical claim, which is the one the design was built for. No cell clears a pre-registered threshold against the distance-matched sham.**
>
> The closest is the **upper** barrier at **percentile 8.4** — the real barrier survives
> somewhat *more* than a sham placed at the same distance, but it does **not** reach the 5th
> percentile, and the **lower** barrier runs the other way at 86.6. **The two sides disagree in
> direction**, which is what noise looks like, and neither of the other two controls comes near
> a threshold either.
>
> `PT-030` §5 row 2 fires: *"Identical survival → **The previous week's extreme is an ordinary
> price at this sample.** Report prominently — it would undercut the mechanism V02 states three
> times."*

Median hours to breach: **upper 38.0, lower 35.5**.

> ### ⚠ `C-001` — a curve is reported and NO day count is adopted.
>
> `PT-030` §3c: *"**No session may read a modal survival time off this curve and record it as
> resolving `C-001`.** The contradiction is about what the instructor *said*; this measures
> what price *did*."* The figures above are **evidence about price, not about the source**.
>
> The censoring is at **~120 hours, not 168** — the curve speaks only to the 5-day trading
> week, and any reading of *"three days"* must be taken against that, not against a calendar
> week. **All of `C-001`'s candidate values (2.5, 3, 3.5, 4 days) lie inside the span**, and
> the curve is published so each can be read off it — **including the possibility that none of
> them matches**, which is what a curve declining smoothly from 0.87 to 0.53 with no shoulder
> looks like.

## 5. MEASURES 3 AND 4 — APPROACH AND DEPTH

| | median | n | §4.1 status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **upper approach** (un-breached only) | **77.4 pips** | 95 | above 30 |
| **upper depth** (breached only) | **76.1 pips** | 83 | above 30 |
| **lower approach** (un-breached only) | **89.3 pips** | 82 | above 30 |
| **lower depth** (breached only) | **100.7 pips** | 96 | above 30 |

**§3b pre-registered that either subset might fall under 30 and carry the descriptive-only
label. Neither did** — the breach/no-breach split is close to even on both sides, so all four
subsets clear the floor comfortably. The contingency is discharged rather than dropped.

> ### **`PT-030` §5 row 3's alternative reading does NOT apply. The breaches are not pokes.**
>
> *"Breach rate high but breach depth tiny → the *'spread to reach a little higher'* reading
> (V02 `[00:24:51]`) becomes the live one."* **Median breach depth is 76 and 101 pips.** A
> barrier crossed by a hundred pips is not being brushed; it is being ignored.
>
> Equally, §1a's expected *near-touch* doctrine — the level approached to within *"three to five
> pips"* — is **not what the un-breached weeks look like either**: the median closest approach
> is **77 and 89 pips**. **Neither the near-miss reading nor the shallow-poke reading survives
> the measurement.**

## 6. DIRECTIONAL CONDITIONING — with the four cell counts, as §3b requires

| | breach rate | count |
|---|---|---|
| net **UP** week / barrier it runs **toward** (last week's high) | **0.690** [0.585, 0.779] | 58/84 |
| net **UP** week / barrier it runs **away from** (last week's low) | **0.345** [0.252, 0.452] | 29/84 |
| net **DOWN** week / barrier it runs **toward** (last week's low) | **0.713** [0.614, 0.794] | 67/94 |
| net **DOWN** week / barrier it runs **away from** (last week's high) | **0.266** [0.187, 0.363] | 25/94 |

**All four cells clear n = 30.** The asymmetry is large and symmetric between the two
directions — a week that travels up breaches the level above it about 70% of the time and the
level below it about 27–35% of the time, and the mirror holds.

> **`PT-030` §5 row 4 calls this *"expected, and reported as a conditioning finding rather than
> folded into one number"*. It is expected in the strongest sense: it is close to a tautology.**
> A week whose net move is upward has, by construction, spent the week going up. The cells are
> reported because the pre-registration requires them and because **the pooled 0.466 / 0.539
> headline conceals a 0.69 / 0.27 spread** — but nothing here is evidence about market-maker
> behaviour.

**A defect found and fixed before this table was read:** the first implementation took the
**upper** barrier as *"toward"* for **both** net directions, which mislabelled the two net-down
cells as each other and reported *"net DOWN/toward = 0.266"* — the rate at which a **falling**
week breaches the barrier **above** it. Corrected; **no number changed, only which label sat on
it**, and the table above is the corrected one.

## 8. RULE APPLICATION VERSUS OUTCOME

The decision point is the **week open**, and the barrier uses **no future data** — it is the
previous week's realised high and low, complete before the tested week begins. **All three
baselines were computed before the real survival curve** (§7 step 5); the run order in
`data/pt030_report.txt` is the print order. **Every exclusion was applied and counted
explicitly**, including §3a″'s **two** (step 4), and the realised 178 / 356 matches the
pre-registration. **The four direction × breach cells are reported with their counts** (step 6).
Neither `PT-030` nor `PT-012` was edited. **No day count was adopted anywhere.**

## 9. WHAT THIS OBSERVATION DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**Does:**
- **Refutes the claim as stated.** V02 states the barrier absolutely; it is breached in
  **83/178** weeks above and **96/178** below. **Only 17 of 178 weeks breach neither.**
- **Finds no level effect.** Against a **distance-matched** sham the real barrier reaches
  percentile **8.4** (upper) and **86.6** (lower) — **the two sides disagree in direction and
  neither clears**. N3 and the donor control agree (25.9 / 35.0 / 27.1 / 64.7). **§5 row 2:
  the previous week's extreme is an ordinary price at this sample.**
- **Rules out both of the softer readings.** Median breach depth **76 / 101 pips** — not a poke;
  median closest approach on un-breached barriers **77 / 89 pips** — not a three-to-five-pip
  near-touch.
- **Publishes a survival curve against `C-001` without adopting any day count**, censored at
  120 hours and declining smoothly with no shoulder at any candidate value.
- Confirms the pre-registered sample exactly — **178 tested weeks, 356 barrier observations** —
  including §3a″'s second exclusion, `2014-06-08`, which nothing in the data flags.

**Does not:**
- Test "peak formation" (`A-010`), which is undefined. The previous week's extreme is a
  measurement and is reported as one.
- **Resolve `C-001`.** No modal survival time may be read off §4's curve as resolving it.
- Support the directional conditioning of §6 as evidence about market makers — it is close to
  tautological.
- Support any execution claim — breaches are **bid-side** with no spread (§0b).
- Permit any barrier price here to be reconciled to an FXCM-sourced level (§0a.1).
- Generalise beyond GBP/USD and 2013-01-06 → 2016-06-30. **The October 2016 flash crash, the
  era's most extreme breach candidate, is in HOLDOUT and was not opened.**
