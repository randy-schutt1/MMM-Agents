### What the amendment added — and it is a disclosure, not a design change

The amendment pins **where the week open falls**: the corpus opens the week at **17:00
exactly**, which on Sundays is the **first bar of window (i)**. Under **Arm B** it moves to
**18:00 during US DST** — one hour *into* the window — and stays 17:00 in standard time. So
the Sunday instance's contamination is **arm- and season-dependent in position**, not merely
present. The separate-reporting rule stands unchanged; what is added is the requirement to
**compare the two arms on the Sunday instance specifically** and report divergence there as a
`D-031` finding.

**No trigger, window, metric, comparator or threshold moved, and the amendment was recorded
before any result was seen.**

### C-6 completeness

A session day enters only with **all 96** of its fifteen-minute slots — which is what
Metric 3 (daily extremes) and every comparator require.

```text
Arm A:  n = 256  (2 excluded — 2015-08-21 DATA, 2015-12-24 CLOSURE)
Arm B:  n = 222  (36 excluded — see §5; 34 of them are the US-DST Mondays)
```

`QA_REPORT.txt` C8's requirement that every flagged session carry an explicit pre-registered
disposition is discharged mechanically: Dec/Jan flagged sessions are **real market closures**
and are still excluded, because a closure cannot support a full-window measurement either.
**Nothing is excluded for being unrepresentative.**

## 5. THE AMENDMENT'S REQUIRED COMPARISON — THE SUNDAY INSTANCE. **THE ARMS DIVERGE HARD**

**Window (i) by the calendar weekday on which it starts** (window (i) of session day D occurs
on the evening of D−1, so the Sunday instance belongs to session day Monday and **is the week
open**):

| Start weekday | Arm A: n · M1 · M2 · M3 | Arm B: n · M1 · M2 · M3 |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 52 · 5.006 · 7.477 · 0.0769 | 52 · 5.366 · 6.813 · 0.0769 |
| Tue | 52 · 5.448 · 7.690 · 0.2500 | 52 · 5.830 · 7.040 · 0.2692 |
| Wed | 51 · 5.590 · 8.327 · 0.0392 | 51 · 6.606 · 7.902 · 0.0980 |
| Thu | 49 · 6.572 · 9.608 · 0.1429 | 49 · 6.923 · 11.469 · 0.1837 |
| **Sun — the week open** | **52 · 8.439 · 8.352 · 0.3654** | **18 · 9.756 · 9.239 · 0.6111** |
| *non-Sunday combined* | 204 · 5.641 · 8.256 · 0.1275 | 204 · 6.168 · 8.262 · 0.1569 |

**Week-open position, measured on each arm rather than assumed:**

| | Arm A | Arm B |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday instances present | **52** | **18** |
| Week open observed at | 17:00 × 52 | 17:00 × 18 |
| US DST Sundays | 34, all present | **0 — all 34 absent** |
| Standard-time Sundays | 18 | 18 |

> ### **The amendment predicted arm- and season-dependent contamination. What the data shows is stronger than that: under Arm B the US-DST Sunday instances DO NOT EXIST.**
>
> Under Arm B during US DST the week open shifts to **18:00**, one hour into window (i). That
> leaves the **17:00–18:00 hour of session day Monday with no bars at all**, so those session
> days fail the 96-slot completeness rule and are excluded. **All 34 US-DST Mondays drop out
> of Arm B** — which is the whole of Arm B's 36-day shortfall against Arm A (256 → 222).
>
> This is a `D-031` **finding, not a defect**, exactly as the amendment requires it be
> reported. Concretely:
>
> - **The Dead Gap's Sunday instance cannot be measured at all on Arm B for two-thirds of the
>   year.** The one-hour clock ambiguity does not merely move the contamination — for 34 weeks
>   of 2015 it removes the observation.
> - **Arm B's Sunday sample is n = 18**, which is **below the n ≥ 30 floor**:
>   `SAMPLE INSUFFICIENT FOR INFERENCE — descriptive only`. Its M1 of 9.756 and M3 of 0.6111
>   may not be quoted anywhere without that label in the same sentence.
> - **Arm B's remaining Sunday instances are a seasonal subsample** — winter only — so the
>   arm-to-arm Sunday difference (8.439 vs 9.756) confounds the clock with the season and
>   **cannot be read as a clock effect**.
>
> **The contamination itself is confirmed and is large.** On Arm A, where all 52 Sundays are
> present, the Sunday instance runs **8.439 pips per bar against 5.641 on non-Sundays — 50%
> higher** — and **36.5% of Sunday session days place a daily extreme inside window (i)
> against 12.8% on other days**. The week open is not a minor perturbation of the Dead Gap; on
> Sundays it dominates it. **`PT-004` §3's separate-reporting rule is doing real work**, and
> the Dead Gap result in §3 holds *because* the Sunday instances are only 52 of 256 — the
> non-Sunday M1 of 5.641 is quieter still than the 6.209 headline.

**Friday, the other half of the pre-registered weekday rule.** There is **no Friday-evening
instance of window (i)**: the week closes at Friday 17:00, so Fri 17:00–20:00 contains no bars
and forms no session day. The Friday effect appears instead in the **BEFORE comparator
14:00–17:00 of session day Friday**, which ends exactly at the weekly close:

| | Arm A | Arm B |
|---|---|---|
| 14:00–17:00, session-day Friday | n = 49 · M1 8.709 · M2 10.757 | n = 49 · M1 8.880 · M2 14.153 |
| 14:00–17:00, all other days | n = 207 · M1 8.468 · M2 12.900 | n = 173 · M1 9.355 · M2 16.524 |

**The final three hours of the trading week are not measurably different from any other
afternoon** on either arm — M1 within 0.5 pips, and M2 actually *lower* into the close.

## 8. WHAT THIS OBSERVATION DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**Does:**
- **The printed 5pm–8pm "Dead Gap" is genuinely quiet on GBP/USD** — N2 percentiles 20.1/31.8
  (M1), 9.6/3.8 (M2), 11.5/23.4 (M3), and quieter than both neighbours in 3 of 4 arm-metric
  cells. **A printed, never-spoken line survives its first empirical test.**
- **The two printed half-hour gaps do not.** (ii) is *busier* than the null on both arms;
  (iii) is busier than the null but is a local dip. **Half-hour resolution is at the limit of a
  15-minute series and this is a resolution limit, not a refutation.**
- **The quietest window of the GBP/USD day is 23:00–00:30, not the Dead Gap.**
- **`C-004`: neither candidate half-hour looks like a gap, and the arms disagree about which
  is quieter.** Recorded as evidence; resolves nothing.
- **The week open dominates the Sunday instance of the Dead Gap** — M1 50% higher, and 36.5%
  of Sunday daily extremes fall inside the window against 12.8% otherwise (Arm A).
- **Under Arm B, all 34 US-DST Sunday instances are unmeasurable** and Arm B's Sunday sample
  falls to **n = 18, `SAMPLE INSUFFICIENT`** — a concrete, quantified cost of `A-019`.

**Does not:**
- Test any trading rule, or *"The MM Spread Is Set"* (`PT-004` §6).
- Resolve `C-004`, or close `A-019`.
- Establish that the Dead Gap is *tradeable* or that quiet hours are safe hours.
- Support reading Arm B's Sunday figures as a clock effect — they are a winter-only
  subsample and are confounded with season.
- Generalise beyond GBP/USD and 2015-01-04 → 2015-12-31.
