>
> PT-017 measures time-to-profit after a **location trigger**, not after the instructor's

### Resolution — finer than the pre-registration asked for, and recorded as `PT-017` §3 requires

`PT-017` §3 specifies *"5-minute preferred, 15-minute fallback… the resolution actually used
is recorded in the observation"*. The corpus is **M1**, so **time-to-profit is measured at
one-minute resolution** — finer than the preferred option, and a strict improvement rather
than a substitution. **The trigger stays on 15-minute closes exactly as pre-registered.**

### The path is measured UNSTOPPED, and that is what the pre-registration asks for

Measure 4 is *"maximum adverse excursion **before** first profit — the number that decides
whether an 18-pip stop (V04 `[00:04:43]`) survives long enough to collect it"*. That question
is only answerable if the stop is **not** applied while measuring — otherwise MAE can never
exceed 18 by construction. So measures 1–4 run on the raw forward path to the 17:00 close,
and the **18/50 version a trader would actually experience is reported beside it in §6, never
instead of it**.

### Baselines — all three run and printed before the trigger population was read

| Arm | What was run |
|---|---|
| **Primary — N1 matched random entry** | Same window, same 03:00–17:00 eligible hours, direction matched, entry bar randomised, **1,000 iterations, seed `20260812`**; reports the same five checkpoints plus both time-to-profit measures and MAE |
| **Second — the natural control** | The same measures on days where **no** qualifying 25–50 excursion occurred, entered at the same clock times drawn from the trigger population's own minute distribution. Isolates the trigger from the hour |
| **Third — random direction** | `D-029`'s secondary arm: is there *any* directional edge at these hours before asking whether this one has it? |

> ### **This is the first result in this batch that clears its pre-registered null, and it clears it on both arms.**
>
> After a 25–50 pip close beyond the Asian box, an away-direction position reaches **+10 pips
> in a median 24 (A) / 27 (B) minutes**, against a matched-random-entry null of **35 / 34.5
> minutes** — **below the 5th percentile of the null on both arms** (percentile 0.0 / 0.4).
> It also reaches +10 pips **at all** more often: 0.778 / 0.786 against a null of 0.624 /
> 0.646, at **percentile 100.0 on both arms**.
>
> The natural control points the same way and more sharply: on days with **no** qualifying
> excursion, entered at the same clock times, the median time to +10 pips is **56 / 74
> minutes** — roughly double. **So the effect is the trigger, not the hour.**
>
> **And the observed median of 24–27 minutes sits inside the instructor's 15–45 minute
> band.** `PT-017` §5's first row — *"Trigger entries reach profit materially faster than N1 →
> Support for the timing half of `CL1`, on a **proxy entry**"* — is the row that fires.

### 4a. What that does and does not license, stated immediately

- It is support for the **timing** half of `CL1`, measured on a **location proxy**, at a
  **+10-pip** definition of "in profit" that the instructor never gave.
- **It is not `CL1`.** His entry is the second-leg close (`A-007`) confirmed by TDI
  (`A-039`); neither is defined in V01–V06, so the full rule stays untestable and this
  result is a **lower bound** on it, not a measurement of it.
- **"Guaranteed" is refuted by construction, and `PT-017` §5 requires this be said
  explicitly.** Any share below 1.000 refutes the word. **22.2% (Arm A) / 21.4% (Arm B) of
  triggered entries never reached +10 pips at all before the 17:00 close.**

| target / stop / open at 17:00 | 66 / 227 / 90 | 65 / 237 / 91 |

> ### **The claim survives as a fact about price and dies as a trade, and the reason is the target, not the stop.**
>
> An 18/50 geometry breaks even at a hit rate of `18 / (18 + 50) = 0.265`. The observed hit
> rates are **0.225 and 0.215** — both below break-even, both with intervals that barely reach
> it. Expectancy is **+0.41 / −0.06 pips before costs**, i.e. zero, and negative once the
> unmodelled 1–2 pip spread is paid.
>
> **Price reaches +10 pips fast and significantly more often than chance; it reaches +50 pips
> less often than the 18-pip stop requires.** `CL1`'s timing has support at a 10-pip
> definition and none at the 50-pip target the instructor's own arithmetic uses.

## 7. `D-031` — THE ARMS AGREE, INCLUDING ON THE POSITIVE RESULT

This is the closest agreement anywhere in the batch so far. Both arms return: trigger on
~75–77% of days; Measure 1 vacuous and slightly below the null; **Measure 2 at percentile
100.0 and 0.0 / 0.4**; no Measure 3 checkpoint above 90.6; Measure 4 at percentile 100.0 with
~4.6% breaching the stop; and a break-even 18/50 trade.

| | Arm A | Arm B |
|---|---|---|
| Median minutes to +10 pips | 24 | 27 |
| Percentile of N1 (time to +10) | 0.0 | 0.4 |
| Ever reaching +10 | 0.778 | 0.786 |
| 18/50 expectancy | +0.41 | −0.06 |

**A one-hour clock shift moves the headline by 3 minutes and does not change a single
verdict.** That matters: `BT_V04_0001` §7 and `BT_V04_0002` §6 both found the arms diverging
materially, so agreement here is informative rather than assumed. **A result about
*elapsed time after a trigger* is far less clock-sensitive than a result about *which hours
carry range*** — which is what one would expect, and it is now measured rather than assumed.

The `D-029` **random-direction** secondary arm sits within noise of the matched-direction
null at every checkpoint (e.g. Arm A t+45: 0.4959 vs 0.4986). **There is no directional edge
at these hours before the trigger is applied**, so Measure 2's result is attributable to the
setup rather than to a standing bias in the window.

## 8. RULE APPLICATION VERSUS OUTCOME

`STUDY_PROTOCOL.md` §2 requires rule application be graded separately from trade result.

- **Rule application: fully mechanical, no discretion exercised anywhere.** The trigger is a
  function of one close and the box; direction is a function of which edge; the checkpoints,
  the +10 threshold, the stop and the target are all fixed in advance.
- **Days excluded: only for coverage, and counted and labelled** (§2). The 129 (A) / 119 (B)
  days that never produced a qualifying 25–50 close are not discarded — they *are* the
  natural control.
- **Losers retained:** 227 / 237 stop-outs are in §6, and 90 / 91 positions still open at
  17:00 are marked to market rather than dropped.
- **`D-009` honoured.** No parameter was adjusted toward 15–45 minutes. The +10-pip threshold
  and all five checkpoints were pre-registered in `PT-017` §3 before any chart was opened,
  which is the only reason §4 can be read as a result rather than as a threshold chosen to
  make a claim land.
- **One measurement defect found and fixed before the numbers were read:** the checkpoint
  code initially reported the last available bar's P&L when a trade had less runway than the
  checkpoint — silently converting a censored observation into a measured one. Checkpoints
  beyond a trade's available horizon are now `nan`, which is why the t+60 and t+120
  denominators are below n.

## 9. WHAT THIS OBSERVATION DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**Does:**
- After a 25–50 pip close beyond the Asian box, an away-direction position reaches **+10 pips
  in a median 24 / 27 minutes** against a matched-random-entry null of 35 / 34.5 —
  **percentile 0.0 / 0.4, clearing the pre-registered null on both `D-031` arms.**
- It reaches +10 pips at all on **0.778 / 0.786** of triggers against a null of 0.624 / 0.646
  — **percentile 100.0 on both arms.**
- The natural control (untriggered days, same clock times) takes **56 / 74 minutes**, so the
  effect is the **trigger**, not the hour.
- **"Guaranteed" is refuted**: 22.2% / 21.4% never reached +10 pips at all.
- **"In profit" at M1 resolution is vacuous** — median 0 minutes for the trigger *and* for
  random entry, with the trigger at percentile 5.3 / 16.2.
- **No fixed-clock checkpoint distinguishes the trigger from chance** (best percentile 90.6).
- The 18-pip stop survives to collect the +10 in ~95% of cases, but the **18/50 trade returns
  +0.41 / −0.06 pips** — break-even before costs, negative after.

**Does not:**
- Confirm or refute `CL1` **as stated**. The instructor's entry is `A-007` + `A-039`, both
  undefined; this is a location proxy and a lower bound (`PT-017` §6).
- License the +10-pip threshold as the course's meaning of *"in profit"*. It is the
  pre-registered operationalisation, and the instructor gave none.
- Support trading this trigger. §6 is the answer to that and it is break-even before costs.
- Close `A-007`, `A-011`, `A-039`, `A-019`, `C-001` or any other record.
- Generalise beyond GBP/USD, 2014-01-05 → 2015-12-31, and the V02 printed box.
