## §1 — WHAT WAS TESTED

V19's central rule, spoken for the M at `[00:22:43]`, mirrored for the W at `[00:46:38]`, confirmed
verbatim on an independent ASR engine, and **written into the deck on camera** at `23:25` / `50:34`:

> *"The second leg rise can be slightly above the first **but must close back below** — **within 30
> minutes**."* … *"If he stays above the previous high … he is gonna extend that high to the next
> level strike zone **25 to 50** higher."*

**Operationalised** (`PT-047` §3, fixed before the run): within a session day's post-box bars, the
**first** bar whose high exceeds a running day-high that is **8–24 bars old** is an *exceed event*;
the level `L` is that prior high. The event is **`CLOSED_BACK`** if either of the next two bars
closes below `L`, else **`HELD_ABOVE`**. `O1` is the maximum excursion above `L` over the following
**16 bars (4 h)**.

**No entry, exit, stop, target, position size or P&L is computed anywhere. This is not a strategy
backtest and must not be cited as one** (`PT-047` §1a).

⭐ **`O4` is the strongest single number in this test and it was not the decision criterion.** The
pre-registration measured the roll-off but declined to score it, because V19 gives no target for it.
**It separates the groups more cleanly than `O1` does, and in the direction the lesson predicts:**
events that closed back below the level subsequently traded **31.85 pips below it** at the median,
against **9.2 pips** for those that held. **The lesson's *"price appears to roll off of the level"*
is visible in the data it was not scored on.**

⚠ **`O3` is the weakest number and it is reported at face value.** Only **a third** of `HELD_ABOVE`
events land *inside* the `[25, 50]` band. **That is not a failure of the rule — most of the misses
are ABOVE 50, not below 25** (`O2` = 76.9 % reach 25+). **The lesson's band describes where the
*next level* sits, not a ceiling**, and this test cannot distinguish "extended to the next strike
zone and stopped" from "extended past it". **The `[25, 50]` band is therefore evidenced as a floor,
not as an interval**, and `PT-047` §5's use of it as an interval is the more demanding test — which
the median passed.

## §4 — ⚠⚠ THE OBJECTION THE PRE-REGISTRATION FAILED TO DECLARE — RAISED, TESTED POST-HOC, AND SURVIVED

**This is the most important section of this report and it is a self-reported defect.**

`PT-047` §2 declared five weaknesses in advance. **It missed a sixth, and it is the most obvious
one:**

> **The classifier's own bars are inside the outcome window.** `HELD_ABOVE` is *defined* as *"price
> did not close back below `L` in the two bars after `t`"*, and `O1` is measured from **`t+1`** —
> so bars `t+1` and `t+2` contribute to **both** the label and the outcome. **Part of the observed
> gap is mechanical, not predictive**, and the permutation test does **not** repair that: `N1`
> destroys the label/outcome association but the *observed* Δ it is compared against is already
> contaminated.

**A pre-registration that had spotted this would have measured `O1` over a disjoint window from the
start. This one did not, and that is a defect in the pre-registration, not in the data.**

### The post-hoc check — ⚠ NOT PRE-REGISTERED, AND LABELLED AS SUCH

`06_MANUAL_BACKTEST/scripts/posthoc047.py` re-measures `O1` over **`t+3` … `t+16` only** — strictly
**after** the 30-minute decision is complete, so the classifier window and the outcome window are
**disjoint**. Nothing else changes.

| arm / window | `nH` | `nB` | med `HELD` | med `BACK` | **Δ pips** | perm `p` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A / `W-A` (primary) | 39 | 60 | 43.20 | 16.30 | **+26.90** | 0.0005 |
| A / `W-B` | 69 | 137 | 35.50 | 12.80 | **+22.70** | 0.0001 |
| B / `W-A` | 36 | 62 | 44.20 | 23.85 | **+20.35** | 0.0044 |
| B / `W-B` | 73 | 134 | 34.30 | 16.85 | **+17.45** | 0.0001 |

⭐⭐ **THE EFFECT DOES NOT SHRINK — IT GROWS SLIGHTLY.** On the primary cell Δ moves from
**+25.75 → +26.90** pips and `p` from 0.0007 → 0.0005. Every cell stays positive and significant.

**Reading, stated conservatively:** the mechanical overlap is **not** what produces the separation.
Bars `t+1`–`t+2` were contributing almost nothing to `HELD_ABOVE`'s excursion — the extension
happens **after** the decision window, which is what the lesson actually claims (*"he will extend"*,
future tense, once the cap has passed).

⚠️ **This check is post-hoc and is reported as post-hoc.** It was run after seeing the pre-registered
result. **It cannot carry the same weight as a pre-registered arm**, and a reviewer is entitled to
discount it. **It is reported because omitting it would have been worse.**

## §5 — WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

**It establishes:** on GBP/USD M15 over 2014–2015 development data, on both `D-031` timezone arms
and both pre-registered windows, whether price closes back below an exceeded 8–24-bar-old day-high
**within 30 minutes** separates two excursion populations by **17–27 pips at the median**, at
`p ≤ 0.004`, with the held-above median landing **inside the lesson's stated `25–50` pip band**, and
the effect **surviving a disjoint-window re-measurement**.

**It does NOT establish:**

* ⚠ **that this is tradeable.** No spread, no slippage, no commission, no execution and no position
  sizing is modelled. `D-006` defers all of that to Phase 8. **An excursion is not a fill.**
* ⚠ **that the M formation works.** The M requires the eight-bar count *and* the *"blue tracer"*
  confirmation, and the tracer is undefined (`A-133`). **This test isolates one clause.**
* ⚠ **that the level definition is the course's.** `PT-047` §2b declared it a convention of this
  test. **A reviewer preferring a swing-pivot or peak-formation level would build a different event
  set, and that is a legitimate attack this report names rather than waits for.**
* ⚠ **anything about the `90 %` at `[00:25:34]` or the `high 80 %` at `[00:28:45]`.** Neither was
  tested; `D-009` and the third-party-anecdote problem are recorded in `Q-020` and
  `V19_INTERPRETATION.md` §2.3.
* ⚠ **anything out of sample.** The `D-035` holdout is sealed and was not touched. `W-A` and `W-B`
  **overlap** (both contain 2015), so `W-B` is a **wider-window** replication, **not** an
  independent one. `PT-047` called it *"out-of-window"*; ⚠ **that phrasing overstates it and is
  corrected here.**
