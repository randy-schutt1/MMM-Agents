<sub>Source: `data/pt035_results.json`, keys `cells.*.n3.d4_median` / `cells.*.n3.d4_ci`. The
runner prints `FAIL` for `A25` and `B15`, `PASS` for `A15` and `B25`, and reports the run as
failed in aggregate.</sub>

### The diagnosis — and the estimator was wrong in a way that is entirely my own

The comparator `(1 − p̂)⁴` is the probability of four consecutive losses **when trades are drawn
independently**. But each sequence has a **fixed composition**: exactly `L` losses in `n` slots.
Under a random permutation of that fixed multiset, the expected fraction of all-loss 4-blocks is

```text
(L/n) x ((L-1)/(n-1)) x ((L-2)/(n-2)) x ((L-3)/(n-3))     <   (L/n)^4
```

— sampling **without** replacement, which is strictly smaller than the i.i.d. product. **So the
i.i.d. comparator over-states the null by exactly that factor, and the shuffled control is
negative by construction.** The magnitude (0.13–0.48 pp at these `p̂` and `n`) is the right order for `n = 200`, and the
**sign is negative in every cell** — which is what the algebra predicts and what chance would
not produce four times out of four.

**`O3`'s clustering difference is therefore not measuring clustering. It is measuring my
comparator's bias plus, possibly, some clustering. The two are not separable in this design.**

### §6 — the verdict that needs no measurement at all, and it is the one that should be quoted

```text

```

### What the claim would need to be, to be true
