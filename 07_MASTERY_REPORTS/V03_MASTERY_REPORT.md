open, and A-019 (session times, no timezone) is still unresolved. On the FXCM feed the
week opened Sunday 21:00 UTC, confirmed from the chart's own crosshair label.

But a measurement is not a rule. It tells you where two lines go, not what to do.

The tradeable object is the second-leg M/W back at a cut level, filtered by the sample
card's eleven criteria. **Six of eleven are undefined**, and the deciding concept — *is
this cut a false move?* — has no stated test. My homework is the evidence: the observable
signature fired 4 of 4 while the outcome it predicts occurred 2 of 4.

### The exit — closest to complete, and still short

> *"If ADR times three is met or not met, but the three day time window is, and you get
> an outside structure high, you better take your money."* `[00:36:11]`–`[00:36:16]`

This has real structure: a target, a time window, a condition, and an explicit statement
of what happens when the target is not met. It is the most rule-shaped sentence in three
lessons. It fails to be testable on one word — **"outside structure" is undefined**
(A-033) — and my measurements put both of its numbers in doubt:

| Taught | Measured, 4 majors, one week |

One week is one week and settles nothing. But it points the same way in 4 of 4, and it is
the first quantitative evidence this project has produced bearing on `C-001`.

**Conclusion: no rule from V03 should be encoded.** The block measurement could be, and
should still wait for A-019.
