## THE RULE

**Never silently reconcile conflicting teachings.**

Picking the tidier reading invents a rule the instructor never taught, and the
invention becomes untraceable. A contradiction may be a timeframe difference, a
market phase, a setup subtype, a later refinement, an instrument difference, a
context, a stated exception, instructor shorthand — or a genuine inconsistency.
All are worth knowing; collapsing them into a guess destroys the information.

## RESOLUTION STATES

`UNRESOLVED` (default, and often correct) · `PROVISIONAL` (working interpretation,
flagged, not written into the concept library as fact) · `RESOLVED` (later material
settles it, cite the evidence) · `HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`.

A **foundational** unresolved contradiction may justify a reviewer `BLOCKED`.
A non-foundational one may permit `PASS` with an open research item.

## REVIEW CADENCE

Re-examine every open record at each cumulative checkpoint (25/50/75/100%). Later
lessons frequently resolve earlier conflicts — and sometimes create ones invisible
within any single lesson.

Ignoring a contradiction is error code E13.
